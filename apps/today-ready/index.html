Today Ready

Turns the weather into four morning answers — umbrella, laundry, outfit, and outdoor plans — conclusion first.

Overview

Today Ready is a weather app that skips the forecast and gives you the answer. Instead of charts and hourly graphs, it turns your local weather into the four calls you actually make every morning: whether to bring an umbrella, whether laundry is better indoors, whether a light jacket helps, and whether outdoor plans are easier earlier in the day.

The design philosophy is 'Open. See. Decide.' — one screen, four decision cards, conclusion first, with the weather data shown only as supporting evidence. There is no radar, no weekly forecast browsing, and no weather news, because the goal is to get you out the door, not keep you reading.

A prep checklist lets you tick items off as you get ready; today's checks stay for the day and reset the next morning. Home Screen widgets keep the top answer visible: the small widget shows the main prep conclusion, and the medium widget adds today's weather summary. Weather comes from Apple Weather/WeatherKit, with Open-Meteo as a fallback so the answers keep working.

Who It Is For

  • Commuters who just want to know if they need an umbrella before leaving home
  • People deciding whether to hang laundry outside or dry it indoors
  • Anyone choosing an outfit or jacket based on today's conditions
  • Parents planning a park trip or short outing around the weather
  • People tired of scrolling detailed forecasts for a simple yes/no answer

Main Features

  • Four decision cards: umbrella, laundry, outfit, and outdoor/park plans
  • Conclusion-first layout — the answer comes before the forecast data
  • Daily prep checklist that resets automatically the next day
  • Small and medium Home Screen widgets showing the top prep conclusion and weather summary
  • Weather from Apple Weather/WeatherKit with Open-Meteo fallback
  • Units options (Auto, Metric, Imperial), compact wind display, manual refresh, light and dark mode

How to Use

  1. Open Today Ready and allow location access so it can fetch your local weather.
  2. Read the four decision cards: umbrella, laundry, outfit, and outdoor plans.
  3. Check off prep items as you get ready — today's checks reset automatically tomorrow.
  4. Pull the latest conditions any time with manual refresh from the header.
  5. Add the Home Screen widget to keep the first answer visible; open the app when the widget asks for a fresh update.
  6. Adjust units (Auto, Metric, Imperial) in the small settings sheet if needed.

Privacy

Today Ready uses your location only to fetch local weather for its decisions, and does not store your location on a server. There is no account, no tracking, and no ads, and the app does not sell personal data. The widget never requests location or fetches weather itself — it only reads the latest decision snapshot the app saved locally in an App Group container.

FAQ

Is Today Ready free?

Yes. It is free to download with no ads and no paid features. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

What does the app do with my location?

Location is used only to fetch local weather so the app can show your umbrella, laundry, outfit, and outdoor decisions. It is not stored on a server, and there is no account or tracking.

Does Today Ready have a widget?

Yes. The small widget shows the top prep conclusion with compact temperature info, and the medium widget adds today's weather summary. Widgets show the latest saved snapshot and may ask you to open the app to refresh.

Where does the weather data come from?

Apple Weather/WeatherKit is the preferred provider. If WeatherKit is unavailable, the app falls back to Open-Meteo so the same local decisions keep working.

Is this a full forecast app?

No, by design. There is no radar, weekly forecast, or weather news — Today Ready shows only the four everyday decisions, with weather data as supporting evidence.

Do my checklist items carry over?

Today's checks stay for the day so you can see what is done, and they reset automatically the next day.
