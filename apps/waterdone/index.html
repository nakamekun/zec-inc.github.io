WaterDone

Log a drink in seconds, see today's water progress, and review daily and monthly hydration history.

Overview

WaterDone is a simple water intake tracker. Log a drink in a couple of taps, watch today's progress fill up, and look back over your recent days and a monthly log view to see how consistent you have been.

It is built for lightweight personal tracking, not medical management: WaterDone does not try to calculate a required hydration target or give health advice. No account is required, and your water logs are stored locally on your device.

A Home Screen widget gives you a faster surface for hydration: it shows your latest water status and supports logging via App Intents, so a quick entry does not require opening the app. Optional daily reminder notifications help you keep the habit going.

Who It Is For

  • People who want a simple daily water log without a full nutrition app
  • Desk workers who forget to drink and want an optional daily reminder
  • Habit builders who like seeing a monthly view of their consistency
  • Anyone who wants quick logging from a Home Screen widget
  • Users who prefer apps with no account and no cloud sync

Main Features

  • Fast water logging in a couple of taps
  • Today's hydration progress at a glance
  • Day logs sheet for reviewing individual entries
  • Monthly log view for spotting streaks and gaps
  • Optional daily reminder notifications
  • Home Screen widget with quick status and App Intent logging
  • Local, on-device storage with no account required

How to Use

  1. Open WaterDone and log a drink when you have one.
  2. Check today's progress on the main screen.
  3. Open the day logs sheet to review or inspect individual entries.
  4. Switch to the monthly view to see your hydration pattern over time.
  5. Optionally set a daily reminder notification in settings.
  6. Add the Home Screen widget to see your latest status and log without opening the app.

Privacy

Water logs, reminder preferences, and widget state are stored locally on your device. No account is required, and no analytics, ads, tracking, or external server storage are used. When you use the widget, the latest log state is shared between the app and widget through a local App Group container on the device. Notifications are optional and used only for the reminder you configure.

FAQ

Is WaterDone free?

Yes. It is free to download, and no in-app purchases are documented for this release. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Where are my water logs stored?

On your device. WaterDone stores logs, reminder preferences, and widget state locally, with no account, no analytics, and no external server storage.

Does WaterDone have a widget?

Yes. The Home Screen widget shows your latest water status and supports quick logging via App Intents, so you can record a drink without opening the app.

Can it remind me to drink water?

Yes. You can turn on an optional daily reminder notification. The app works fine without notifications if you prefer.

Does it calculate how much water I should drink?

No. WaterDone is a lightweight personal log, not medical advice — it deliberately does not try to compute a required hydration target.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored locally, so you can log and review your history without an internet connection.
