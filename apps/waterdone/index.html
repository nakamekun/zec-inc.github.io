Overview

WaterDone is the lightest way to keep sipping before you get thirsty. Had a sip? Tap once. There are no amounts to type and no daily target — the goal is a rhythm: another sip within the interval you choose. A little drop dries out as time passes and fills up when you drink, and a gentle word tells you how you are doing, like "Hydrated" or "Time for a sip".

The home screen shows when your next sip is due, with a ring around the drop, and today's sips appear on a single timeline. You can log with one tap from a Lock Screen or Home Screen widget without opening the app, and undo a mistaken tap in one tap. A streak and a simple calendar keep every sip on record.

A gentle nudge arrives before you dry out. You pick the interval (2–4 hours) and the hours it can reach you, with quiet hours and a daily reminder cap, and the app works fine with notifications off too. Optionally, WaterDone can log each sip to Apple Health. No account is required and your logs stay on your device. WaterDone is not medical advice and does not calculate how much water you need.

How to Use

  1. Open WaterDone, or use the Lock Screen or Home Screen widget, and tap once when you have a sip.
  2. Check on the home screen when your next sip is due and how hydrated you are.
  3. Tapped by mistake? Undo it in one tap.
  4. Open today's timeline to see your sips, and the calendar to see past days and your streak.
  5. Choose your reminder interval (2–4 hours), the hours it can reach you, and the daily cap in settings, or leave notifications off.
  6. Optionally turn on logging to Apple Health.

Privacy

Water logs, reminder preferences, and widget state are stored locally on your device. No account is required, and no analytics, ads, tracking, or external server storage are used. When you use the widget, the latest log state is shared between the app and widget through a local App Group container on the device. Notifications are optional and used only for the reminders you configure. Apple Health logging is optional and off by default; when you turn it on, WaterDone only writes your sips to Water and never reads Health data.

FAQ

Is WaterDone free?

Yes. It is free to download, and the store listing mentions no in-app purchases. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Where are my water logs stored?

On your device. WaterDone stores logs, reminder preferences, and widget state locally, with no account, no analytics, and no external server storage.

Does WaterDone have a widget?

Yes. You can log a sip with one tap from a Lock Screen or Home Screen widget without opening the app.

Can it remind me to drink water?

Yes. A gentle nudge arrives before you dry out. You choose the interval (2–4 hours) and the hours it can reach you, and you can set quiet hours and a daily reminder cap. The app works fine without notifications if you prefer.

Does it calculate how much water I should drink?

No. There are no amounts to type and no daily target. WaterDone is a lightweight rhythm tracker, not medical advice — it deliberately does not calculate how much water you need.

What if I tapped by mistake?

You can undo it in one tap.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

Optionally. Logging to Apple Health is off by default; when you turn it on, WaterDone writes each sip to Water and never reads your Health data.

Does it work offline?

Logs and history are stored on your device and WaterDone has no account or external server storage, so you can log and review your history without an internet connection.
