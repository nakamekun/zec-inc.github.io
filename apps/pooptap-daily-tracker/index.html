Overview

PoopTap is a one-tap poop tracker with no ads, no account, and no subscription. Pick the shape and color right on the home screen, then tap Record Log once. Tapped by mistake? Undo within 8 seconds. Your log lands on a private calendar on your device, your streak grows, and your poop buddy evolves through stages from Tiny Poop to Legendary, while the Throne Keeper comments on your progress, whether you asked or not.

Growth fits on one screen, and tapping your buddy brings a bit of toilet trivia once a day. In Dress up, every look shows how to unlock it, and the empty throne is a look you can keep; future forms stay a surprise until you earn them. Only a limited daily growth bonus counts toward evolution, so consistency is rewarded, not spam.

Under the humor is a useful bowel movement log: shape and color, optional symptom marks, a short note per entry, a monthly calendar view, and optional reminders when your record goes quiet. A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget lets you log without opening the app, and the large inner display on iPhone Duo is supported. PoopTap is for personal tracking and entertainment. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How to Use

  1. Install PoopTap from the App Store.
  2. After you go, pick the shape and color on the home screen and tap Record Log, or log from the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget.
  3. Tapped by mistake? Tap Undo within 8 seconds.
  4. Add optional symptom marks or a short note when useful.
  5. Review your history on the monthly calendar, check your buddy's growth, and change its look in Dress up.
  6. Optionally turn on reminders or Apple Health linking, and export a backup, in settings.
  7. Use PoopTap as a personal log, not as medical advice or analysis.

Privacy

Your logs stay on your device. PoopTap has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. If you turn on Apple Health linking, PoopTap writes only the constipation, diarrhea, and bloating marks you choose; it does not infer symptoms or read Health data. A backup is created only when you export one, and you choose where to save it with the share sheet. Check the App Store product page for Apple's current privacy label.
