PoopTap: Poop Tracker & Log

A one-tap poop tracker and bowel movement log with no ads and no account. Private calendar, optional symptom marks, and a poop buddy that grows as you log.

Overview

PoopTap is a one-tap poop tracker with no ads, no account, and no subscription. Tap once after you go, and your log lands on a private calendar on your device. Your streak grows, and your poop buddy evolves through stages from Tiny Poop to Legendary, while the Throne Keeper comments on your progress, whether you asked or not.

Under the humor is a useful bowel movement log: shape and color details, optional symptom marks, a short note per entry, a monthly calendar view, and optional reminders when your record goes quiet. A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget lets you log without opening the app. Only a limited daily growth bonus counts toward evolution, so consistency is rewarded, not spam.

PoopTap is for personal tracking and entertainment. It is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Who It Is For

  • People who want a simple daily bowel movement tracker or stool log
  • Users who want a private poop diary with no account and no ads
  • People who want to keep shape, color, symptom marks, or short notes about digestion and constipation for their own reference
  • Users who prefer a playful, habit-building log over a medical-style app

Main Features

  • One-tap logging, including from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget
  • Shape and color details, optional symptom marks, and a short note per entry
  • Monthly calendar view of your history and a growing streak
  • A poop buddy that evolves from Tiny Poop to Legendary, watched over by the Throne Keeper
  • Optional reminders when your record goes quiet
  • Optional Apple Health linking that writes only the constipation, diarrhea, and bloating marks you choose
  • Free iCloud Backup to your own iCloud Drive, with restore after reinstalling
  • Optional PoopTap Plus one-time purchase: extra evolution forms, premium trivia, extra achievements, premium share cards, Annoying reminder mode, themes, and discreet app icons

How to Use

  1. Install PoopTap from the App Store.
  2. Tap after you go to add a quick log, or use the Home Screen or Lock Screen widget.
  3. Add optional shape, color, symptom marks, or a short note when useful.
  4. Review your history on the monthly calendar and watch your poop buddy evolve.
  5. Optionally turn on reminders, iCloud Backup, or Apple Health linking in settings.
  6. Use PoopTap as a personal log, not as medical advice or analysis.

Pricing

PoopTap is free to download and use, with no ads and no subscription. iCloud Backup is free. PoopTap Plus is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. App Store pricing and availability may vary by country and can change over time.

Privacy

Your logs stay on your device. PoopTap has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. If you turn on Apple Health linking, PoopTap writes only the constipation, diarrhea, and bloating marks you choose; it does not infer symptoms or read Health data. If you use iCloud Backup, a copy of your logs and settings is saved to your own iCloud Drive. Check the App Store product page for Apple's current privacy label.
