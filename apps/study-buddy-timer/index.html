Overview

Study Buddy Timer is a simple study timer. Tap Start when you begin, tap Stop when you are done — no session length to set up. The timer keeps running even if you close the app, and you can pause anytime. Daily study time, session counts, and totals show how your hours add up.

What makes it different is the buddy: every minute you study earns EXP and levels up a laid-back companion that chats when you tap it, and can even call you by the name you set (the name stays on your device and never appears in notifications). Taking a break never shrinks your buddy. It is deliberately a study timer first and a character second — the timer, elapsed time, and totals stay front and center, and the buddy is light motivation rather than a game.

You can time your study without opening the app: the elapsed time and your buddy appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and you can start and stop from a Home Screen widget, Control Center, the Action button, an NFC tag on your desk (via Shortcuts), or a Focus. Beyond the timer there are study plans, Pomodoro, ambient sounds, a daily goal, exam countdowns, subject charts, CSV export, and free iCloud sync and backup.

With Study Pair you connect with one friend or family member through an invite link and grow one buddy together, with no rankings. Days when both of you do your part become pair days, and a partner can choose to cheer instead of study. Your partner sees only study-time totals, never your subjects, notes, or when you studied.

Main Features

  • A buddy that grows with every minute you study, talks when you tap it, and can call you by name — it never shrinks when you rest
  • Study plans (including pasting a plan made with AI, calendar events, and a Reminders list), subjects, and textbooks
  • Pomodoro, daily study-time goal, a one-line current goal, exam countdown with reminders, and ambient sounds that unlock as your buddy levels up
  • Summary card after each session, focus rating, study calendar, weekly review, weekly and monthly charts by subject, and CSV export
  • Free iCloud sync and backup: switch iPhones and get your sessions and buddy back with the same Apple ID
  • Study Pair: grow one buddy with one partner, with pair days and a cheer role; creating a pair needs Study Buddy Premium, and invited partners join for free

Who It Is For

  • Students who want to log study hours without setting up a timer each time
  • Working adults studying for certifications or exams
  • Friends or family who want to study together, or cheer someone on, without rankings
  • People building a study habit who respond to small progress rewards
  • Anyone who finds full task managers too heavy for simple time tracking

How to Use

  1. Open Study Buddy Timer and tap Start when you begin studying (choose a subject or plan first if you like).
  2. Study — the elapsed time is shown live, including on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and the timer keeps running if you close the app.
  3. Tap Stop when you finish; the session is saved, you earn EXP, and a summary card shows today's total and your streak.
  4. Check your daily study time, charts, calendar, and your buddy's level, and tap your buddy to chat.
  5. Add a Home Screen widget or a Control Center control to start and stop without opening the app.
  6. To study with someone, create a Study Pair and send the invite link.

Privacy

There are no ads. Your study records are saved on your iPhone and never sent to the developer's servers, and the app works without an account. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also saved in your own iCloud, which the developer cannot see. Data shared in a Study Pair is stored in the pair creator's iCloud, and your partner sees only study-time totals — not when you studied, your subjects, or your notes. The name your buddy calls you stays on your device. For the authoritative summary of data practices, see the privacy label on the app's App Store page.

FAQ

Is Study Buddy Timer free?

Yes, it is free to download, and the timer, records, buddy, and iCloud sync are free. Study Buddy Premium is an optional monthly or yearly subscription for extras such as Apple Watch timing, past weeks and months, creating a Study Pair, and more exams. If you bought Study Buddy Pro before, you keep all its benefits without subscribing. Check the App Store listing for current details.

Where is my study data stored?

Study records are saved on your iPhone and are not sent to the developer's servers. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also saved in your own iCloud. See the App Store privacy label for the full summary of data practices.

Is iCloud sync free?

Yes. iCloud sync and backup are free for everyone. When you switch iPhones, turn on sync with the same Apple ID to get your sessions and buddy back.

Does it have widgets?

Yes. A Home Screen widget lets you start and stop and see your study time without opening the app. While the timer runs, the elapsed time and your buddy also appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can also start and stop from Control Center or the Action button.

How does the buddy grow?

Every minute you study earns EXP, which levels up your buddy, and the app shows how far it is to the next level. Taking a break never shrinks your buddy. Tap your buddy to chat; if you set a name in Settings, it sometimes calls you by it.

What is Study Pair?

Connect with one partner through an invite link and grow one buddy with the time you both put in. You can see each other's study time today and whether the other person is studying right now. On days when both of you do your part, it's a pair day and your buddy grows more. You can choose a cheer role instead of studying. Your partner sees only study-time totals. Only the person who creates the pair needs Premium; invited partners join for free.

Do I have to set a timer length before starting?

No. It works as a stopwatch: tap Start when you begin and Stop when you finish, so there is no barrier to just starting. Pomodoro mode is also available if you prefer 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of rest.

What happens if I close the app during a session?

The timer keeps running even if you close the app. If you forget to stop, you get one reminder after 3 hours, and you can adjust the end time later.

Is it a game?

No. It is designed as a practical study timer first — the buddy is a light motivational touch, not a full pet-raising game, and there are no rankings.
