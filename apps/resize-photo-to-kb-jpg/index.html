Overview

Prepare photos for forms and uploads by adjusting their file size, pixel dimensions, and crop. Choose a HEIC, PNG, or JPG photo, set your requirements, and check the finished JPG. HEIC and PNG photos are saved as new JPG copies; your originals stay unchanged.

Set a file size limit Choose 200KB, 500KB, 1MB, 2MB, or 4MB, or enter a custom limit. Photo Resize adjusts JPEG quality and, when needed, reduces pixel dimensions to stay within the limit. The result shows its actual file size and pixel dimensions before you save.
