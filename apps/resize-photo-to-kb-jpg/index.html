Overview

Photo Resize prepares a photo for forms and uploads by adjusting its file-size limit, pixel dimensions, and crop. Choose HEIC, PNG, or JPG, then verify the finished JPG's actual file size and dimensions before saving; the original remains unchanged.

Choose a preset or custom size limit, keep dimensions, set the long edge, or enter width and height. Crop freely or use common ratios, then save to Photos or Files, share, or drag the JPG into another app. Location is removed and capture date is kept unless you turn that off.

One photo at a time and one named preset are free. Photo Resize Pro adds batch processing with the same settings and more than one preset. The interface is available in 25 languages and photos can open from the share sheet or an action.

Main Features

  • Preset or custom file-size limit with actual result size shown
  • Keep dimensions, set long edge, or specify width and height
  • Free crop and 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9, or specified-dimension ratio
  • JPG export to Photos, Files, sharing, or drag and drop
  • Original unchanged; location removed and optional capture-date removal
  • One photo and one preset free
  • Pro subscription for batch processing and multiple presets
  • On-device processing, no ads; interface available in 25 languages

Who It Is For

  • People uploading photos to forms with file-size limits
  • Users converting HEIC or PNG to JPG
  • Anyone who needs exact pixel dimensions or a crop ratio
  • People processing several photos with the same settings (Pro)

How to Use

  1. Choose a HEIC, PNG, or JPG photo.
  2. Set the size limit, pixel dimensions, and crop.
  3. Check the actual finished file size and dimensions.
  4. Save or share the new JPG.

Pricing

All size, dimension, crop, JPG export, save, and share tools are free for one photo at a time, with one named preset and no ads. Photo Resize Pro is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription for batch processing and multiple presets. Any trial terms appear on the purchase screen. Check the App Store for current terms.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

Photos are processed on the device and are not sent to a server. Originals remain unchanged. Export removes location information and keeps capture date unless you choose to remove it in Settings.

FAQ

Can it guarantee an upload will be accepted?

No. It shows the finished size and pixels, but you must compare them with the receiving service's requirements.

Does it check passport-photo rules?

No. It does not assess requirements such as face size.

What is free?

All tools for one photo at a time and one named preset are free. Pro adds batch processing and multiple presets.
