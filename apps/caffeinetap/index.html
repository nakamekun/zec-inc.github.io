CaffeineTap

Log caffeine in one tap and see how many mg you can still drink today - with a widget and cutoff reminder.

View on the App Store All ZEC Apps

Overview

CaffeineTap is a lightweight caffeine tracker built to answer one question fast: how much caffeine can I still drink today? Tap coffee, espresso, tea, cola, or an energy drink and the app updates your daily total, your remaining amount against your personal limit, and a simple pace status.

It deliberately skips what heavier caffeine apps pile on - no accounts, no complicated charts, no HealthKit, no half-life math, no sleep predictions. Just quick logging, a seven-day history, and a small Home Screen widget showing today's remaining caffeine at a glance.

A single optional notification, the Cutoff Reminder, nudges you at the time you choose so you can pause before drinking caffeine late in the day.

Who It Is For

  • Coffee drinkers who want to stay under a daily caffeine limit without effort
  • People trying to cut back on afternoon caffeine for better evenings
  • Energy drink and tea drinkers who lose track of how much they've had
  • Anyone who abandoned heavyweight caffeine apps and just wants a quick log
  • People who want their remaining caffeine visible on the Home Screen

Main Features

  • One-tap logging with presets: coffee (95 mg), espresso (65 mg), black tea (45 mg), green tea (30 mg), energy drinks (80/160 mg), cola (40 mg)
  • Custom amount entry for anything else
  • Personal daily limit in mg, with today's total, remaining amount, and a pace status (Good pace, Moderate, Near your limit, Over your daily limit)
  • Seven-day history grouped by day, with entry deletion
  • Small Home Screen widget showing today's remaining caffeine, or 'Over limit' when exceeded
  • Cutoff Reminder - a single local notification at the time you set
  • Local storage only; no account, no HealthKit

How to Use

  1. Set your personal daily caffeine limit in Settings.
  2. When you drink something, tap its preset - coffee, espresso, tea, energy drink, cola - or enter a custom amount.
  3. Check the home screen for today's total, how many mg you have left, and your pace status.
  4. Add the small widget to your Home Screen to see remaining caffeine without opening the app.
  5. Optionally enable the Cutoff Reminder in Settings to get a nudge at your chosen time.
  6. Review the last seven days in History and delete entries if you mis-tapped.

Privacy

CaffeineTap does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data - no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no accounts, no server sync. Caffeine entries, your daily limit, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device, and the widget reads the same local data. The app does not use HealthKit. The Cutoff Reminder is a local notification, and permission is requested only if you enable it.

FAQ

Is CaffeineTap free?

Yes. The app is free with no in-app purchases or paid features. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Where is my caffeine data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, no server sync, no analytics, and no ads. The widget reads the same local data through the app's App Group.

Is there a Home Screen widget?

Yes, a small widget that shows how many mg of caffeine you have left today (or 'Over limit'), along with your total versus your daily limit.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

No. CaffeineTap does not use HealthKit. It is a standalone personal log.

Will it send me notifications?

Only one, and only if you turn it on: the Cutoff Reminder at a time you choose. There are no logging-prompt notifications, and permission is asked only after you enable the feature in Settings.

How accurate are the caffeine amounts?

Preset amounts are estimates and can vary by brand, size, and brewing method. You can enter a custom amount when you know the exact figure. The app is for personal tracking and does not provide medical advice.

Can I see past days?

Yes. History shows the last seven days grouped by day, and you can delete individual entries.
