Animal Face Hide: Kids Photos

Cover faces in your photos with cute animal stamps - cat, bear, bunny and more - before you post. Detects faces automatically; runs on your device.

View on the App Store All ZEC Apps

Overview

Animal Face Hide covers faces in your photos with cute animal stamps. Open a photo and the app finds the faces and places a cat, bear, bunny and more on each one. Tap a face you want to show to remove its stamp, then save or share the covered photo.

Unlike blur or mosaic tools, animal stamps keep the photo cheerful - especially handy before you post kids' photos to social media, a family album, or a school group. Choose from cat, bear, bunny, squirrel, dog, panda, and chick, or let "Mix" give each face a different animal. You can also use headwear such as cat ears, bunny ears, bear ears, and a knit hat, or subtle covers like frosted glass and a patch for name tags and addresses.

Everything happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded, your original photo stays as it is, and the covered photo is saved as a new image with no location data from the original. Face detection can miss faces in profile or very small faces, so check the whole photo before you save or share.

How to Use

  1. Open the app and pick a photo.
  2. The app finds the faces and places an animal stamp on each one. Choose a different animal, headwear, or cover style if you like.
  3. Tap a face you want to show to remove its stamp, or place a stamp by hand where you need one.
  4. Move, resize, and turn stamps with your fingers; undo one step or clear everything if you change your mind.
  5. Check the whole photo, then save it as a new image or share it. Your original photo is untouched.

FAQ

Is Animal Face Hide free?

Yes. The app is free on the App Store with no ads. Pricing can change, so check the store listing for current details.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device and nothing is uploaded.

Does it change my original photo?

No. The original stays as it is. The covered photo is saved as a new image.

Does the saved photo include my location?

No. Saved images contain no location data from the original photo, so you can share them without exposing where they were taken.

How is this different from blur or mosaic apps?

Instead of blurring, the app covers faces with cute animal stamps - cat, bear, bunny, and more - so the photo stays cheerful, which is especially nice for kids' photos.

Can I adjust a stamp after placing it?

Yes. Move, resize, and turn stamps with your fingers. You can undo one step or clear everything, and you can place stamps by hand if no faces are found.

Does it catch every face?

Not always. Face detection can miss faces in profile or very small faces, so check the whole photo before you save or share it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-up.
