Cute Privacy Stamp

Hide faces and private details in photos with cute stamps - hearts, stars, cats - before you share.

Overview

Cute Privacy Stamp covers faces and private details in photos with cute stamps before you post, sell, or send them. Pick a photo, place a heart, star, cat, or sparkle over what you want to hide, and save or share a freshly rendered copy.

Unlike blur or mosaic tools, stamps keep the photo cheerful - especially handy before posting kids' photos to social media. It is a focused, single-purpose editor: no filters, no beauty editing, no layer system, just stamps you can move, resize, and rotate with your fingers.

Everything happens on your device. Your original photo is never modified, nothing is uploaded, and the saved copy contains no location metadata from the original.

Who It Is For

  • Parents who want to hide their kids' faces before posting to social media
  • People selling items online who need to cover names, addresses, or other private details in listing photos
  • Anyone sharing group photos who wants to cover other people's faces politely
  • Users who find blur and mosaic too plain and want photos to stay cute
  • Privacy-conscious users who don't want photos uploaded to a server for editing

Main Features

  • Cute stamps for hiding faces: hearts, sparkles, stars, cats, and more
  • Privacy mark stamps such as NO, NG, HIDE, PRIVATE, SECRET, STOP, COVER, and MASKED
  • Eye-bar stamps and large cover patches for broader areas
  • Drag to move, pinch to resize, and two-finger rotate for each stamp
  • Undo the most recent stamp or clear all stamps
  • Save or share a newly rendered copy - the original photo stays unchanged
  • Saved copies contain no location metadata from the original photo

How to Use

  1. Open the app and pick a photo with the photo picker.
  2. Choose a stamp - a heart, star, cat, sparkle, eye bar, or a privacy mark like HIDE or PRIVATE.
  3. Drag the stamp over the face or detail you want to cover, then pinch to resize and rotate with two fingers.
  4. Add more stamps as needed; undo the last one or clear all if you change your mind.
  5. Save the stamped copy to your library or share it directly - your original photo is untouched.

FAQ

Is Cute Privacy Stamp free?

Yes. The app is free on the App Store with no ads. Pricing can change, so check the store listing for current details.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens on your device. The app does not upload photos to a server and does not use external APIs for image processing.

Does it change my original photo?

No. The original stays untouched. Saving or sharing creates a newly rendered copy with the stamps applied.

Does the saved photo include my location?

No. The saved copy contains no location metadata from the original photo, so you can share it without exposing where it was taken.

How is this different from blur or mosaic apps?

Instead of blurring, you cover faces and details with cute stamps - hearts, stars, cats, sparkles - so the photo stays cheerful, which is especially nice for kids' photos.

Can I adjust a stamp after placing it?

Yes. Drag to move it, pinch to resize, and rotate with two fingers. You can undo the most recent stamp or clear them all.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account system, no sign-up, and no tracking.
