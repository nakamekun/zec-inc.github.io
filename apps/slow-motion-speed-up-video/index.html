Overview

Change an existing video's speed, preview the timing, and save to Photos or share it. Constant-speed exports from 0.25× to 4× are free and carry no watermark, with no app-imposed duration limit.

Keep audio pitch, mute audio, or let pitch follow the speed. Source dimensions, frame rate, orientation, and supported HDR are kept. Speed Curves set different speeds for individual sections with smooth transitions; free curve exports carry a small watermark.

Pro removes the curve watermark and expands the range to 0.1× through 16×. Slow motion repeats frames rather than creating interpolated frames, and export re-encodes the video. Everything is processed on device with no account, ads, tracking, or upload. The app opens videos from the share sheet and actions and is available in 25 languages.

Who It Is For

  • People making a whole clip slower or faster
  • Creators changing speed in different sections
  • Users who want pitch-preserving audio or mute
  • Privacy-conscious users who want on-device video processing

How to Use

  1. Choose or share a video into the app.
  2. Pick a constant speed, or create a speed curve by section.
  3. Choose how audio pitch should behave and preview the result.
  4. Export to Photos or share the new video.

Pricing

Constant speeds from 0.25× to 4×, audio options, and watermark-free constant-speed exports are free. Free Speed Curve exports include a small watermark. Pro is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription that removes the curve watermark and unlocks 0.1× to 16×. Check the App Store for current terms.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

Video processing stays on the device. The app requires no account and has no ads, tracking, or media upload. Export re-encodes the new video while leaving the source unchanged.

FAQ

Does the free version add a watermark?

Not to constant-speed exports. Free Speed Curve exports include a small watermark, which Pro removes.

Does slow motion create new interpolated frames?

No. It repeats frames when needed.

Are videos uploaded?

No. Processing stays on your device.
