DailyAlbum: One Photo a Day

Keep one photo a day as a simple photo diary and memory calendar, then hang it in a frame: a spare iPhone, iPad, or your TV shows today's photo. Free optional iCloud sync.

Overview

DailyAlbum keeps one photo a day and hangs it in a frame, without turning it into a photo management task. Open a date, choose one photo that captures the day, and the daily choices grow into a memory calendar.

It fits everyday moments, your own life, trips, plants, creative work, pets, and family. Empty days can even fill themselves: DailyAlbum quietly suggests one good photo, favoring people and pets and skipping screenshots and document shots, with all processing done on the device.

Each day can hold a note of up to 300 characters, and you can search notes later to find a day again. Frame Mode shows today's photo full screen with its date and note, so a spare iPad or an old iPhone becomes a photo frame for your room, and you can also show it on a TV with AirPlay or an HDMI adapter. At the start of each month, replay last month as a monthly album, and save or share the monthly recap as a vertical video for free. On iPhone Duo, a full month sits on the left and the selected day's photos on the right.

With the optional DailyAlbum Premium subscription, you can also invite family to view one shared photo per day in DailyAlbum; viewing is always free for them. Full details, guides and the video are on the app's own site: https://dailyalbum.app/

Main Features

  • One-photo-a-day memory calendar, with a monthly album replay at the start of each month
  • On-device photo suggestions that can fill empty days automatically
  • Notes up to 300 characters per day, with note search
  • Frame Mode: today's photo full screen with its date and note, on a spare iPhone or iPad, or on a TV via AirPlay or HDMI
  • Share any day as a card with its photo, date, and note, and save the monthly recap as a vertical video for free
  • Home Screen and StandBy widgets and gentle 'on this day in past years' notices
  • Free optional iCloud sync that restores your records after a phone change or reinstall, plus a free ZIP export of every day's photo and note
  • Wide two-pane view on iPhone Duo: a full month on the left, the selected day's photos on the right
  • Optional DailyAlbum Premium subscription: Archive, unlimited Frame Mode and monthly album replays, photo copy sync, year images, extra designs, and family sharing (view-only and free for family)

Who It Is For

  • Parents and families who want a gentle daily photo habit for kids, pets, and everyday moments
  • People who want to remember ordinary days privately, without posting to social feeds
  • Travelers and hobbyists who want one representative photo per day
  • Anyone who wants a widget or a photo frame at hand to revisit saved days

How to Use

  1. Install DailyAlbum from the App Store.
  2. Open a date and choose one photo that captures the day.
  3. Add a short note if you want (up to 300 characters per day).
  4. Turn on iCloud Sync (free, optional) so your records survive a new phone or a reinstall.
  5. Add the widget, or open Frame Mode on a spare iPhone, iPad, or TV, to revisit saved days without opening the app.
  6. Share a favorite day as a card - photo, date, and note - or a month as a recap video, whenever you choose to.

Pricing

The core app is free: the calendar, photo picking, notes, note search, iCloud sync for choices and notes, and saving the monthly recap as a video. DailyAlbum Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) that adds Archive, unlimited Frame Mode, unlimited monthly album replays, photo copy sync, year images and extra designs, and lets invited family view one shared photo per day; viewing is always free for them. Plus is no longer sold, and people who already own it keep all their Plus benefits permanently. See the App Store for current prices and availability.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

No account is required. By default DailyAlbum shows photos straight from your photo library and does not upload them. With iCloud Sync on (free), your day choices and notes are stored in your personal iCloud. Only if you turn on photo sync or share with family is a copy of just those photos kept in your own iCloud too — never on the developer's servers, and never readable by the developer. Full policy: https://dailyalbum.app/privacy/

FAQ

What is DailyAlbum for?

DailyAlbum keeps a one-photo-a-day record of everyday life, family moments, trips, hobbies, and projects, arranged as a memory calendar you can revisit.

Is DailyAlbum a full photo library manager?

No. DailyAlbum focuses on choosing and remembering one photo per day. It does not replace the Photos app or a full album system.

Does DailyAlbum post my photos publicly?

No. There is no social feed and nothing is published automatically. When you want to share a day, you create a card with the photo, date, and note, and share it yourself.

How much of DailyAlbum is free?

The calendar, photo picking, notes up to 300 characters, note search, iCloud sync for choices and notes, and saving the monthly recap as a video are free. Frame Mode is free for 5 minutes at a time. DailyAlbum Premium, an optional subscription, adds Archive, unlimited Frame Mode, unlimited monthly album replays, photo copy sync, year images and extra designs, and family sharing.

Where are my photos stored?

DailyAlbum uses photos from your iPhone photo library. When you choose a photo for a day, DailyAlbum remembers which photo you chose — it does not copy the photo itself. If the original is deleted from the Photos app, DailyAlbum may no longer be able to show it, unless it was saved with DailyAlbum Archive. When iCloud Sync is on, your choices and notes are stored only in your personal iCloud.

Will my records survive a new iPhone?

Yes, if you turn on iCloud Sync. iCloud Sync is free for everyone. Its purpose is simple: your photo choices and notes exist only in DailyAlbum, so this keeps them safe when you switch phones or reinstall the app. How to use it: open Settings, and under iCloud Sync turn on "Sync choices & notes" (you need to be signed in to iCloud). On a new iPhone, turn it on again and your records come back. Everything is stored only in your personal iCloud — photos themselves are never uploaded; they follow your photo library transfer, such as iCloud Photos.

Does restore happen automatically?

If your new iPhone was set up from a backup or Quick Start, iCloud Sync stays on and your records reconnect automatically — nothing to do. After a fresh install, turn iCloud Sync on once in Settings; everything after that is automatic, and there is no separate restore button. This works whenever your records are gone from the device but still in your iCloud: a new phone, a reinstall, or a reset device.

What's the difference between the two iCloud Sync options?

iCloud Sync keeps your day choices and notes safe in your personal iCloud, so they survive a new phone or reinstall and appear on your other devices — for free. The photos themselves aren't uploaded; each device shows them from its own photo library. If you use iCloud Photos, that's usually all you need: the photos you keep are already on your other devices, so even a spare iPad can become a photo frame at no extra cost. If you don't use iCloud Photos, turn on Sync this day's photo. For a little iCloud storage, DailyAlbum keeps a private copy of each day's photo in your own iCloud, so your days still appear on your other devices — and an old iPhone or iPad can become a family photo frame. Only you can open them; the developer cannot read your data. Photo sync is included with DailyAlbum Premium, or with Plus if you already own it.

What happens if I delete a photo from Photos?

If you delete the original photo from the Photos app, it may no longer be available in DailyAlbum. Photos saved with DailyAlbum Archive will still be shown.

What does DailyAlbum Premium include?

DailyAlbum Premium is one optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) for your own album and optional family sharing. It includes Archive (a copy of each day's chosen photo saved inside DailyAlbum), unlimited Frame Mode (the free version plays for 5 minutes at a time), unlimited monthly album replays, photo copy sync, year images and extra designs. Invited family can also view one shared photo per day. The calendar, photo choices, notes and iCloud sync for choices and notes stay free. Manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple Account settings.

What if I already own DailyAlbum Plus?

Plus is no longer sold, but if you already own it, all your Plus benefits stay yours permanently. New Premium benefits require a subscription.

How does family sharing work?

Family sharing is part of DailyAlbum Premium. You choose what to share before inviting anyone: all daily photos, or only days with tags you pick. Then send the invite link. Family members open it on an iPhone or iPad with DailyAlbum installed and see one photo per day with its date. It's view-only and always free for them, and you can stop sharing at any time in Settings.

What is DailyAlbum Archive?

DailyAlbum Archive saves a copy of the photo you chose for each day inside DailyAlbum. Even if the original is later deleted from your photo library, DailyAlbum can still show the saved copy. It also makes browsing steadier and faster: archived copies display instantly, even when the original lives in iCloud and would otherwise need to download first. You can choose whether auto-picked photos are saved too, and see everything that's been saved in Settings > Archived photos. Archive is included with DailyAlbum Premium, or with Plus if you already own it.

Does DailyAlbum Archive copy my entire photo library?

No. DailyAlbum Archive saves only the daily photos you choose so they can be displayed more reliably inside the app. If DailyAlbum is deleted or the device is erased, photos saved only inside DailyAlbum may also be lost.

What do the star and sparkles icons mean?

The star means you picked the photo yourself. The sparkles mean DailyAlbum suggested or picked the photo automatically for that day.

How does DailyAlbum pick photos automatically?

Auto-pick only fills days that are still empty — it never replaces a photo you chose. It looks for photos with people or pets, and skips videos, screenshots, and shots that look like documents or receipts. If a day has only that kind of photo, the day stays empty on the calendar and you can pick one yourself. Everything is analyzed on your device; nothing is uploaded. To change an auto-picked photo, open the day and choose another — the sparkles turn into a star.

Can I use an iPad or old iPhone as a photo frame?

Yes. Open Frame Mode to show your chosen photo full screen with its date and note; the controls fade away after a few seconds, leaving just the photo. Stand an iPad or a spare iPhone on a shelf and it becomes a family photo frame. Frame Mode is free for 5 minutes at a time; DailyAlbum Premium removes the time limit and adds a sleep schedule, so the frame goes dark at night and wakes by itself in the morning.

Can I show the frame on a TV?

Yes. Connect your iPhone or iPad to a TV with AirPlay or an HDMI adapter, then open Frame Mode. The TV shows only the framed photo with its date and note, while your device works as the remote.

Can I search my notes?

Yes. Open your saved days and use the search bar to find a note by a word you wrote. This is free for everyone.

What's the difference between notes and tags?

A note is a short text shown together with the photo — on the day screen, in Frame Mode, and on share cards. Tags are short labels, like a family member's name, that are not shown on the photo; they work behind the scenes for search and for choosing which photos Frame Mode shows. Once you create a tag it is saved, so next time you can add it with one tap instead of typing it again. You can see, rename, and delete all your tags in Settings > Tags.

Can I share a day or a month?

Yes. From a day, tap the share button to create a card with that photo, its date, and note. A month can be shared as one calendar image, and the monthly recap can be saved and shared as a vertical video — both free. DailyAlbum Premium adds a whole year in one image and more designs. Sharing happens through the normal share sheet — nothing is posted automatically, and DailyAlbum never uploads your photos in the background.

How do I restore my purchase?

In Settings, open your plan and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account as before. This restores existing Plus purchases as well as active subscriptions.

Why does the widget show a different photo depending on the widget size?

That's the Memories widget: it picks a random saved day on its own for each size, so a small, medium, and large widget next to each other can show different photos. If you'd rather see the same photo everywhere, use the Today's Photo widget — it shows your photo of the day in every size.

Why can I keep only one photo per day?

DailyAlbum is designed around choosing one meaningful photo for each day. Keeping one photo makes your calendar simple, easy to look back on, and focused on the photo that best represents the day.

Why do photos sometimes take time to load?

DailyAlbum reads photos available on your device. Loading may take a little time when there are many photos to show, when the image is large, or when the app is opening that day’s photos for the first time.
