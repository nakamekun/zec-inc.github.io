Overview

年齢プロフィール (Age Profile) is a Japanese family profile book for iPhone. Save the birthdays of parents, grandparents, children, and relatives, and see at a glance each person's current age, kazoedoshi (traditional East Asian age counting), birth date and current year in wareki (Japanese era calendar: Meiji, Taisho, Showa, Heisei, Reiwa), Chinese zodiac animal, star sign, common yakudoshi (traditionally inauspicious years) guidance, and longevity celebrations such as kanreki (60th) and koki (70th).

Questions like "How old is grandpa this year?", "When is my yakudoshi?", or "Which year is the kanreki celebration?" are answered in one place, without looking up conversion tables. A built-in converter switches dates between the Western calendar and wareki across all five modern eras — handy for Japanese resumes, application forms, and paperwork at government offices — and correctly handles the boundary dates where one era changes to the next.

Everything is stored on your device: no account, no ads, and no data sent to external servers. The app presents yakudoshi and celebration years as common cultural guidance only — it makes no claims about fortune, misfortune, or health.

  • People planning kanreki, koki, kiju, and other longevity celebrations for parents and grandparents

Main Features

  • Family profile book: save birthdays with blood type and notes per person
  • Shows full age, kazoedoshi (counted age), Chinese zodiac, and star sign for each profile
  • Wareki display and converter covering Meiji, Taisho, Showa, Heisei, and Reiwa, with correct era-boundary handling
  • Yakudoshi guidance by gender (male, female, or unspecified), including major yakudoshi (taiyaku)
  • Longevity celebration detection based on kazoedoshi, such as kanreki and koki
  • Notifications for birthdays and longevity celebrations
  • All data stored on device — no account, no ads, no external transmission

How to Use

  1. Add family members and relatives with their birth dates using the date picker
  2. Open the profile list to see each person's age, wareki dates, zodiac, and star sign at a glance
  3. Check the yakudoshi and longevity celebration guidance shown for each profile
  4. Set notifications so you do not miss birthdays or milestone celebrations like kanreki
  5. Use the converter tab to switch any date between the Western calendar and wareki for forms and paperwork

FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes, it is free to download and use, with up to 2 saved profiles in the free version. A one-time Plus purchase (not a subscription) unlocks unlimited profiles. Check the App Store listing for current pricing.

Where is my family's data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the app uses no ads, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

What is wareki and which eras does the app support?

Wareki is the Japanese era calendar used on official documents. The app converts dates across the five modern eras — Meiji, Taisho, Showa, Heisei, and Reiwa — and correctly handles the dates where eras change.

What are yakudoshi and kazoedoshi?

Yakudoshi are years traditionally considered inauspicious in Japan, which differ for men and women. Kazoedoshi is the traditional age counting where you are 1 at birth. The app shows both as common cultural guidance, without any claims about fortune or misfortune.

Can it remind me about birthdays and celebrations?

Yes. You can set notifications for birthdays and for longevity celebrations such as kanreki (60th) and koki (70th).

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app uses no external APIs, so everything works without an internet connection.

Is this a fortune-telling app?

No. Yakudoshi and celebration years are shown only as widely used cultural reference points. The app makes no statements about luck, misfortune, health, or religious effect.
