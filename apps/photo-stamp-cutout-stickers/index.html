Overview

Photo Stamp Cutout cuts the subject out of one photo and finishes it as a transparent PNG in six styles: sticker, postage, rubber stamp, badge, speech bubble, or tag. Add a short label such as OK, LOVE, or SOLD, enter your own text, and choose a color.

Stickers you save, copy, or share collect in a Sticker Book. Sort them into groups and save or share a whole group at once. You can add the photo's date, choose one subject when several are found, change the size, omit text, select among three fonts and top or bottom placement, and copy the finished sticker for pasting elsewhere.

The original photo is never changed. Cutout and finishing happen on your device, with no account, ads, or tracking. The app interface is available in 25 languages.

Main Features

  • Automatic subject cutout, with a tap to choose one subject when several are found
  • Six templates: sticker, postage, rubber stamp, badge, speech bubble, and tag
  • Custom short text, ready-made labels, four colors, three fonts, top or bottom placement, size slider, and no-text option
  • Photo date treatment, using today's date when the photo has no capture date
  • Sticker Book with groups and whole-group save or share
  • Transparent PNG export to Photos, Files, sharing, or Copy
  • Original remains unchanged; all processing stays on device
  • No account, ads, or tracking; interface available in 25 languages

Who It Is For

  • Marketplace sellers adding SOLD or a short note to a listing photo
  • Journal and message users making reusable stickers
  • Pet owners making postage-style keepsakes
  • Anyone who wants transparent PNG cutouts without uploading a photo

How to Use

  1. Choose a photo and, if needed, tap the subject you want.
  2. Pick one of the six templates.
  3. Set the label, custom text, color, font, placement, size, and optional date.
  4. Save, copy, or share the transparent PNG; it is also kept in the Sticker Book.

FAQ

Does it change my original photo?

No. It creates a new transparent PNG and leaves the original unchanged.

What styles are available?

Sticker, postage, rubber stamp, badge, speech bubble, and tag.

Can I reuse stickers?

Yes. Saved, copied, and shared stickers collect in the Sticker Book and can be sorted into groups.

Are photos uploaded?

No. Processing happens on your device, with no account or tracking.
