Overview

Blur Before Share hides faces, names, addresses, license plates, and anything else you do not want to show, right before you share a photo. Draw with your finger over the parts to hide, pick an effect, and save or share the result. Your original photo is never changed.

Pick the effect that fits what you are hiding: blur to softly hide faces and backgrounds, mosaic with large pixel blocks that keep the photo looking natural, or a solid black cover, the surest choice for text. You can mix all three in one photo. When you open a photo, Cover Faces finds faces on your device and covers them automatically; tap the × to uncover any one of them.

The copy you save or share is re-rendered from scratch, so it carries no location (GPS) or camera metadata from the original. There is no account, no upload, and no ads. Every tool is free, and you can save or share up to 3 blurred photos a day for free. Blur Before Share Pro removes the daily limit. The app is available in 25 languages.

Who It Is For

  • People posting screenshots that contain names, chats, or account details
  • Parents sharing photos of their kids who want other faces hidden

How to Use

  1. Open the app and pick a photo.
  2. Faces are found on your device and covered automatically; tap × to uncover any you do not want hidden.
  3. Draw with your finger over faces, names, addresses, or anything else to hide, and choose blur, mosaic, or black.
  4. Mix effects, or use undo and reset until it looks right. Touch and hold to compare with the original.
  5. Save the copy or share it directly.

FAQ

Is Blur Before Share free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no ads, and you can save or share up to 3 blurred photos a day for free. An optional Pro subscription removes the daily limit. Check the App Store listing for current details.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your device. There is no account and no upload.

Which effect should I use for text?

Black is the surest choice for text. Blur softly hides faces and backgrounds, and mosaic uses large pixel blocks that keep the photo looking natural.

Does it change my original photo?

No. Your original photo is never modified; the app saves or shares a new copy.

Does the shared copy include location data?

No. The copy is re-rendered from scratch, so it carries no location (GPS) or camera metadata from the original.

Does it automatically detect faces?

Yes. When you open a photo, Cover Faces finds faces on your device and covers them automatically. Tap × to uncover any one of them, or tap to search again. You can also draw over anything else with your finger.

What does Pro do?

Blur Before Share Pro removes the daily limit of 3 saved or shared photos. It is a yearly subscription with a 1-week free trial, or a monthly one. People who used the app before version 1.4 keep unlimited saving and sharing without Pro.
