Why This App

Blur Before Share is for people who want to cover faces and personal information in photos or short videos on their iPhone before sharing them. It combines blur, mosaic and solid black covers in one app, with processing on your device.

For a single opaque patch on a photo, iPhone Markup can be enough. Blur Before Share automatically covers detected faces in photos and lets you place a cover on a moving face in a video, then use Follow Face and review the result before export. Videos can be up to three minutes long.

There is no account, upload or advertising. Shared photos contain no original location or camera metadata, and exported videos omit location data. All tools are free, with three saves or shares per day; Pro removes that limit. Use solid black for sensitive text and check the whole result before sharing.

Main Features

  • Three effects: blur, mosaic, and black, mixed freely in one photo
  • Draw over the parts to hide with your finger
  • Cover Faces finds and covers faces automatically on your device; tap × to uncover any one, or search again
  • Undo and reset (Undo even brings everything back after a reset)
  • Touch and hold to compare with the original
  • Saved and shared copies contain no location or camera metadata
  • Your original photo is never modified
  • No account, no upload, no ads; everything runs on your device
  • Free to use up to 3 saved or shared photos a day, with an optional Pro subscription for no daily limit
  • Available in 25 languages

How to Use

  1. Open the app and pick a photo.
  2. Faces are found on your device and covered automatically; tap × to uncover any you do not want hidden.
  3. Draw with your finger over faces, names, addresses, or anything else to hide, and choose blur, mosaic, or black.
  4. Mix effects, or use undo and reset until it looks right. Touch and hold to compare with the original.
  5. Save the copy or share it directly.

Who It Suits

Best for

  • People covering faces before sharing family photos or short videos.
  • People blacking out names, addresses or numbers in screenshots.
  • People who want on-device editing and shared copies without location data.

Not for

  • For one opaque patch in a photo, use iPhone Markup with a fully opaque colour.
  • For clips longer than three minutes, use a video editor that supports the clip length. For voice changes or automatic subtitles, consider TrackBlur.
  • For batch face blurring across many photos, consider Watermarkly’s batch photo tools.
  • For editing on a computer, use a browser tool such as Facepixelizer.
  • If you only want to remove location data, use a metadata-removal app or the photo-sharing location options.

Pricing

Blur Before Share is free to download and has no ads. Every tool is free, and you can save or share up to 3 blurred photos a day for free. Blur Before Share Pro removes the daily limit, with a yearly subscription that includes a 1-week free trial or a monthly one; people who used the app before version 1.4 keep unlimited saving and sharing without Pro. Check the App Store listing for current prices and details.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device: no account, no upload, no ads. The copy you save or share is re-rendered from scratch, so it carries no location (GPS) or camera metadata from the original, and your original photo is never modified. Faces are found on your device.

FAQ

How do I blur a face in a video on iPhone?

Choose a video up to three minutes long in Blur Before Share, place an oval or rectangular cover on the face and tap Follow Face. Play through the result and check the coverage before exporting. The audio is retained.

How can I pixelate or black out personal information in a screenshot before sharing it?

Open the screenshot, draw over the information with your finger and choose mosaic or solid black. Use an opaque black cover for names, addresses and other sensitive text. Check the full image before saving a copy.

Is there a face blur app that works offline without uploading my photos?

Blur Before Share processes photos and videos on your device, without an account, upload or ads. Faces detected in photos are covered automatically; review the photo and cover anything else by hand.

Can pixelated or blurred text be recovered, and how should I hide text in an image?

Do not treat blur or mosaic as a guarantee that text cannot be inferred. For sensitive text, use a solid black cover over the entire area and inspect the saved copy before sharing.

How do I remove location data from a photo or video before posting it?

Blur Before Share creates a new photo copy without the original GPS or camera metadata and exports video without location data. The original photo is unchanged. Check for visible clues such as addresses as well.

How do I pixelate a child’s face in a photo before sharing it?

Open the photo and review the automatic face covers. Choose mosaic, remove any unwanted cover with ×, and draw over any missed area before saving or sharing.

Is there a free app to blur faces in photos and videos on iPhone?

Every tool is free, with up to three saves or shares a day. The optional monthly or yearly Pro subscription removes that limit, and eligible annual subscribers may receive a one-week trial. Current conditions appear in the App Store.
