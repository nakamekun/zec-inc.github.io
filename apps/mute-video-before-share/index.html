Overview

Mute Video Before Share removes the audio track from a video so you can share it without background voices, conversations, or noise. Pick a video, tap once, and get a silent copy ready to post or send — while your original video stays completely unchanged. You can also choose Mute & Share straight from the share sheet to make a silent copy without opening the app, or choose up to 20 videos, mute them in order, and save or share them all together.

If you only need to hide part of the sound, choose Part and remove, lower, or beep out just the parts you pick, using the sound's waveform to spot where people are talking. The preview sounds exactly the way the copy will, and after the copy is made you can tap Edit Again to adjust it with the same settings. On a wide unfolded iPhone Duo screen, the video and the editor sit side by side.

Free copies get a small watermark in the corner. With Mute Video Pro (an optional subscription), copies have no watermark and keep the video exactly as it is, with no re-encoding, so the picture quality stays the same and even long videos finish fast. The silent copy contains no location or camera metadata from the source video. The app is available in 25 languages and works in portrait and landscape on iPhone, and on iPad.

How to Use

  1. Open the app and choose a video from your Photos library (or up to 20 for batch mute), or choose Mute & Share from the share sheet in another app.
  2. Preview the selected video.
  3. Remove all the sound, or choose Part to remove, lower, or beep out just the parts you pick.
  4. Preview the result — it sounds the way the copy will. Tap Edit Again if you want to change it.
  5. Save it to Photos or share it directly with the share sheet.

FAQ

Is Mute Video Before Share free?

Yes. The app is free with no ads, and removing audio, Part, beep, and the share sheet are free. Free copies get a small watermark in the bottom right; Mute Video Pro, an optional subscription, removes it. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Why is there a watermark on my copy?

In the free version, copies you make get a small watermark (the app's icon and name) in the bottom right. Your original video never gets one. With Mute Video Pro, copies have no watermark. If you used the app before version 1.5, no watermark is added.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Audio removal happens entirely on your device. There is no account, no upload, no server processing, and no tracking.

Does removing the audio reduce video quality?

Copies without the watermark (Mute Video Pro) copy the video track as-is without re-encoding, so the picture quality is identical to the original and even long videos finish fast.

Is there a video length limit?

There is no length limit when you mute one video at a time. Batch mute supports videos up to 60 seconds each; longer videos are skipped and the rest continue.

Can I mute several videos at once?

Yes. Choose up to 20 videos. They are muted one at a time in the order you selected them, and you can save or share them all together.

Can I mute only part of a video?

Yes. Choose Part to remove, lower, or beep out just the parts you pick. The waveform helps you spot where people are talking.

Can I hear it before making the copy?

Yes. With Part, play the preview: removed parts go silent, lowered parts get quieter, and beeps play, just as in the copy. After the copy is made, tap Edit Again to change it with the same settings.

Can I use it from the share sheet?

Yes. Choose Mute & Share from the share sheet to make a silent copy without opening the app.

Does it change my original video?

No. The app creates a new silent copy and leaves your original video untouched in your library.

Does the silent copy include location data?

No. The silent copy contains no location or camera metadata from the original, which makes it safer to share publicly.
