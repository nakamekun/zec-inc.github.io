Mute Video Before Share

Remove all sound from a video in one tap — no re-encoding, no quality loss, no length limit, no watermark.

Overview

Mute Video Before Share removes the audio track from a video so you can share it without background voices, conversations, or noise. Pick a video, tap once, and get a silent copy ready to post or send — while your original video stays completely unchanged.

Unlike video editors that re-encode everything, this app copies the video track as-is and simply drops the audio. That means zero picture-quality loss, fast results even on long videos, no length limit, and no watermark. It is a single-purpose tool that does one job well.

The silent copy is also cleaner than the original in one important way: it contains no location or camera metadata from the source video, so you are not leaking where a clip was filmed when you share it.

Who It Is For

  • People posting videos to social media who want background conversations muted
  • Parents sharing kids' videos without household voices or names in the audio
  • Sellers adding videos to marketplace listings without ambient noise
  • Anyone sending clips to work, school, or family who wants privacy in the soundtrack

How to Use

  1. Open the app and choose a video from your Photos library.
  2. Preview the selected video.
  3. Tap Remove Audio and wait a moment while the silent copy is created on your device.
  4. Preview the silent copy to confirm it looks right.
  5. Save it to Photos or share it directly with the share sheet.

FAQ

Is Mute Video Before Share free?

Yes. The app is free with no ads and no watermark. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Audio removal happens entirely on your device. There is no account, no upload, no server processing, and no tracking.

Does removing the audio reduce video quality?

No. The video track is copied as-is without re-encoding, so the picture quality of the silent copy is identical to the original.

Is there a video length limit?

No. Because the video is not re-encoded, even long videos finish fast.

Does it change my original video?

No. The app creates a new silent copy and leaves your original video untouched in your library.

Does the silent copy include location data?

No. The silent copy contains no location or camera metadata from the original, which makes it safer to share publicly.
