Overview

Mute Video Before Share removes the audio track from a video so you can share it without background voices, conversations, or noise. Pick a video, tap once, and get a silent copy ready to post or send — while your original video stays completely unchanged. You can also choose Mute & Share straight from the share sheet to make a silent copy without opening the app, or choose up to 20 videos, mute them in order, and save or share them all together.

If you only need to hide part of the sound, choose Part and remove, lower, or beep out just the parts you pick, using the sound's waveform to spot where people are talking. The preview sounds exactly the way the copy will, and after the copy is made you can tap Edit Again to adjust it with the same settings. On a wide unfolded iPhone Duo screen, the video and the editor sit side by side.

Free copies get a small watermark in the corner. With Mute Video Pro (an optional subscription), copies have no watermark and keep the video exactly as it is, with no re-encoding, so the picture quality stays the same and even long videos finish fast. The silent copy contains no location or camera metadata from the source video. The app is available in 25 languages and works in portrait and landscape on iPhone, and on iPad.

How to Use

  1. Open the app and choose a video from your Photos library (or up to 20 for batch mute), or choose Mute & Share from the share sheet in another app.
  2. Preview the selected video.
  3. Remove all the sound, or choose Part to remove, lower, or beep out just the parts you pick.
  4. Preview the result — it sounds the way the copy will. Tap Edit Again if you want to change it.
  5. Save it to Photos or share it directly with the share sheet.

Privacy

Everything runs on your device: no account, no upload, no server processing, no ads, and no tracking. Photos access is used only to select videos and save the silent copies. As a bonus, the exported silent copy carries no location or camera metadata from the original video.

FAQ

How do I mute a video on iPhone before sharing it?

Open Mute Video Before Share, tap Choose Video, then tap Remove Audio. You get a silent copy you can Save to Photos or Share right away; the original stays as it is. From the Photos share sheet you can also choose Mute & Share without opening the app.

How do I remove the sound from only part of a video, or bleep out a word?

Switch from Whole Video to Part, tap Add Part Here on the timeline, and choose Remove, Lower, or Beep for that part. Play the preview to hear it the way the copy will sound, then Save or Share.

How do I lower the volume of a video instead of muting it?

Choose Part, add a part over the loud section, and pick Lower. The Volume slider goes from 1% to 100% of the original, and the preview plays at that level.

Can it remove just the music, voices, or background noise?

No. The app removes, lowers, or beeps the whole audio for the time you pick; it cannot separate music, voices, or noise from each other. To silence someone talking, use Part and remove only those seconds.

How do I find where a child's name is said in a video?

In Part, tap Find Spoken Names. The video is transcribed on your device, and spots that sound like a name or place are marked on the timeline. Nothing is removed automatically: listen, then tap Remove This Part. The transcript is not saved.

How do I mute multiple videos at once?

Choose up to 20 videos, then tap Remove Audio from All Videos. They are muted in the order you picked them, and you can Save All to Photos or Share them together. Videos longer than 1 minute are skipped; mute those one at a time, which has no length limit.

Can I mute a screen recording, and can the copy be an MP4?

Yes. Any video in your Photos library, including screen recordings, can be chosen. In Settings, File Format lets you pick Same as Original or MP4 for the silent copy.

Is Mute Video Before Share free? Why is there a watermark?

Yes. Removing audio, Part, Beep, the waveform, and the share sheet are free, with no ads. Free copies get a small watermark (the app's icon and name) in the bottom right; Mute Video Pro, an optional subscription, removes it. If you used the app before version 1.5, no watermark is added. Check the App Store listing for current prices.

Is my video uploaded? Does it keep location data or change the original?

No. Everything runs on your device with no account or upload. The app makes a new copy and leaves the original untouched, and the copy carries no location, shot date, or device info from the original.

Does removing the audio reduce video quality?

Copies without a watermark (Mute Video Pro, or people who used the app before 1.5) copy the video track as-is without re-encoding, so the picture is identical to the original and even long videos finish fast. Free copies are re-encoded to add the watermark.
