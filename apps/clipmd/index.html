Overview

ClipMD turns web pages and copied text into clean Markdown clips you keep on your device.

Paste a URL or share a page to ClipMD, and it extracts the article body and converts it to Markdown - headings, lists, and links included. Copy the result into your notes app, or keep it in ClipMD as a reading archive.
