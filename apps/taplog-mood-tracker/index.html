Moodwash – Feelings in Color

Moodwash is a mood diary that turns mixed feelings into a watercolor of your day: pick several feeling words or none, log as often as you like, and revisit each day in color.

View on the App Store All ZEC Apps

Overview

Moodwash (formerly TapLog) is a mood diary that lets mixed feelings become a painting of your day. Choose several feeling words at once, from happy and grateful to anxious and drained, or leave a moment wordless as a gentle wash of color. Log as often as you like, for free, and revisit each day's painting in your calendar and Recent.

Your daily mood still takes one tap, with five familiar mood buttons. Notes, reminders, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, a Face ID lock with Touch ID or passcode, and CSV export of dates, daily moods and notes are all free. No scores or averages are shown, and Moodwash does not diagnose, treat or promise health benefits.

No account is needed and entries are stored on your device. Optional Apple Health integration saves daily moods and supported momentary feelings to State of Mind and shows sleep, steps, daylight and cycle marks alongside your days. An optional Moodwash Pro subscription adds weather, temperatures, pressure and day length beside your moods.

Who It Is For

  • People who feel more than one thing at once and want a mood diary that allows it
  • Anyone who wants to log a moment without finding the right words
  • Anyone who quit heavier mood trackers because logging took too long
  • People doing lightweight self-reflection who want to spot patterns without scores or averages
  • People who like seeing weather and pressure next to their moods (optional Pro)

How to Use

  1. Open Moodwash and tap one of the five mood buttons for your daily mood.
  2. Or choose several feeling words that fit this moment, or none at all, to log it as a wash of color.
  3. Optionally add a short note for context.
  4. Log as often as you like during the day.
  5. Revisit each day's painting in your calendar and Recent.
  6. Optionally set a reminder, add a widget, or turn on the Face ID lock in Settings.

FAQ

Is Moodwash free?

Yes. It is free to download, and logging feelings as often as you like, notes, reminders, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, the Face ID lock and CSV export are free. An optional Moodwash Pro subscription adds weather beside your moods. Check the App Store listing for current prices.

What happened to TapLog?

TapLog is now Moodwash. Your daily mood still takes one tap, and you can now also choose several feeling words at once or log a moment with none.

Where are my mood entries stored?

Entries are stored on your device, and the app requires no account. See the App Store privacy label for the full summary of data practices.

Do I have to write something every time?

No. A daily mood takes one tap, and you can log a moment with several feeling words or with none at all. Notes are optional.

Can Moodwash remind me to log my mood?

Yes. The app includes reminders to help you build a check-in habit.

Can I look back at past entries?

Yes. Each day's painting appears in your calendar and in Recent, and you can export dates, daily moods and notes as CSV.

Does it connect to Apple Health?

Optionally. When you turn it on, Moodwash saves daily moods and supported momentary feelings to State of Mind and shows sleep, steps, daylight and cycle marks alongside your days.

What is Moodwash Pro?

Moodwash Pro is an optional subscription that shows weather, temperatures, pressure and day length beside your moods. Weather uses your chosen town or, optionally, your approximate location. Monthly and yearly plans are available, and you can cancel in App Store subscription settings.

Does it analyze my mental health?

No. Moodwash shows no scores or averages, and it does not diagnose, treat or promise health benefits. It is a simple mood diary for self-reflection.
