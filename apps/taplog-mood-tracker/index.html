TapLog - Mood Tracker

Record your mood with one tap, add an optional note, and review your recent history — no journaling overhead.

Overview

TapLog - Mood Tracker is a mood diary reduced to its essentials: tap the mood that matches how you feel, and you are done. If you want context, you can add a short journal note — but you never have to, which is what makes it fast enough to keep up every day.

Most mood apps pile on charts, scores, and analysis. TapLog goes the other way: a quick check-in, a simple history, and a journal view for the entries where you added words. It is a tool for noticing how you feel over time, not a mental health diagnosis or treatment app.

Optional reminder settings help you build the check-in habit, and the history view makes it easy to look back over recent days for lightweight self-reflection.

Who It Is For

  • People who want a simple mood diary without long-form journaling
  • Anyone who quit heavier mood trackers because logging took too long
  • Users doing lightweight self-reflection who want to spot patterns over time
  • People who occasionally want to jot a note about their day, but not every day

Main Features

  • One-tap mood check-in
  • Optional journal notes for entries that need context
  • History view of your recent moods
  • Journal view for reading back your written entries
  • Reminder settings to help build the check-in habit
  • Deliberately simple: no complex analytics or scoring

How to Use

  1. Open TapLog and tap the mood that matches how you feel right now.
  2. Optionally add a short journal note for context.
  3. Repeat whenever you want to check in — it takes seconds.
  4. Review the history view to see your recent moods at a glance.
  5. Open the journal view to read back the entries where you wrote notes.
  6. Optionally set a reminder so you remember to check in.

FAQ

Is TapLog free?

Yes. It is free to download and has no in-app purchases. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Where are my mood entries stored?

Entries are kept on your device, and the app requires no account or sign-up. See the App Store privacy label for the full summary of data practices.

Do I have to write something every time?

No. A mood check-in is just one tap. Journal notes are optional, for the days you want to add context.

Can TapLog remind me to log my mood?

Yes. The app includes reminder settings to help you build a daily check-in habit.

Does it analyze my mental health?

No. TapLog is a simple mood memo and history tool for self-reflection. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or mental health analysis.

Can I look back at past entries?

Yes. The history view shows your recent moods, and the journal view lets you read back the entries where you added notes.
