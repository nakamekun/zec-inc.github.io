Overview

ExifOff checks what a photo reveals and creates a clean copy for safer sharing. Pick a photo to see whether it contains location data, along with basics like date, camera, size, and file type - then create a new JPEG copy with the metadata removed before you post or send it.

It is a single-purpose privacy tool, not a photo editor or a full EXIF browser: it shows only the fields that matter for a sharing decision, and it never touches your originals. Cleaning happens entirely on your device with no account, no upload, and no tracking.

You can clean up to 50 photos at once, and even clean straight from the share sheet - share photos from any app to ExifOff and get clean copies back.

Who It Is For

  • Anyone posting photos to social media who doesn't want to reveal where they were taken
  • People selling items online who share photos taken at home
  • Parents sharing kids' photos and worried about embedded location data
  • Users sending photos to strangers via marketplaces, forums, or chat apps
  • Privacy-conscious users who want metadata stripped on-device, not by a web service

How to Use

  1. Open ExifOff and pick a photo (or select up to 50 at once).
  2. Review what the photo reveals: whether location data was found, plus date, camera, size, and file type.
  3. Tap to create a clean copy - a new JPEG without the source metadata.
  4. Save the clean copy to your photo library or share it straight from the app.
  5. Alternatively, share photos to ExifOff from any app's share sheet and get clean copies back without opening the app first.

FAQ

Is ExifOff free?

Yes. The app is free with no ads and no in-app purchases. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Metadata checking and cleaning run entirely on your device. There is no account, no upload, and no tracking.

Does it delete or change my original photos?

No. ExifOff always creates a new clean JPEG copy for saving or sharing. Your originals are never edited or deleted.

What metadata does it remove?

The clean copy is created without carrying over the source photo's metadata, including location data. Before cleaning, the app shows whether location data was found plus the date, camera, size, and file type.

Can I clean multiple photos at once?

Yes. You can clean up to 50 photos in one go.

Can I use it without opening the app?

Yes. Share photos from any app to ExifOff via the share sheet and it hands back clean copies.

Why should I remove EXIF data before sharing?

Photos can carry embedded location coordinates and other details about when and how they were taken. Stripping that metadata before posting or sending prevents strangers from learning where a photo was taken.
