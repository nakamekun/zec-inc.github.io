Big Text Note

Overview

Big Text Note shows one note in large, easy-to-read text so you can hold it up to the person in front of you. It works as a simple communication board: for talking with someone who is deaf or hard of hearing, for days when your own voice is not available, for noisy places like stations and event halls, or for a quick word at a counter or reception desk.

Type it, show it, and if needed, let the device say it. Read Aloud speaks the note with the voices built into your iPhone or iPad, and voice input turns what the other person says into text on the device. Audio is never saved; only the text you keep is stored.

Show mode makes the note a clear message board with adjustable size and colors, an upside-down flip for the person across the table, and landscape for one long line. Quick Phrases keeps four ready-made lines and up to 20 of your own, one tap away. It works with no ads, no account, and no internet connection, and the app is available in many languages.

Who It Is For

  • People who talk with someone who is deaf or hard of hearing and want a communication board
  • People whose own voice is not available, for example after surgery or when unwell
  • Anyone who needs to get a short message across in a noisy place such as a station or event hall
  • People who want a quick word at a counter or reception desk, or a note to show while traveling

How to Use

  1. Open Big Text Note and type the note you want to show, or use voice input to turn the other person's words into text.
  2. Open Show mode to display the note in large text. Pick Normal, Large, or Huge, then fine-tune the size and choose colors.
  3. Flip the note upside down for the person across the table, or turn your iPhone sideways for one long line.
  4. Use Read Aloud if you want the device to say the note, or tap a Quick Phrase for a ready-made line.
  5. Add your own Quick Phrases (up to 20) and reorder them so the ones you use most are on top.

FAQ

How do I show text in big letters on my iPhone?

Type a note and tap Show. The note fills the screen in Normal, Large, or Huge text, in the Text and Background colors you pick, and the screen stays on while it is shown. Turn the iPhone sideways to show one long line.

How do I use my iPhone as a communication board with someone who is deaf or hard of hearing?

Write what you want to say and tap Show so they can read it; tap the up-and-down arrows to flip the note toward them. To get their side, tap the microphone and their speech is turned into text on your device. Ready-made Quick Phrases such as "I'm hard of hearing" and "Please write it down" are one tap away.

How do I see what someone is saying as text, like captions?

Tap the microphone, and the words heard so far appear in the note as they speak; tap the stop button when they finish. Speech is recognized on the device and the audio is not saved. It is start-and-stop, not an always-on caption.

Can my iPhone speak what I typed when I cannot use my voice?

Yes. Tap Read note aloud on the show screen. It uses the voices built into your iPhone or iPad, reads in the language of the note, and sends nothing to a server. If you have set up a Personal Voice, turn on Use a voice close to my own in Settings.

How do I flip the text so the person across the table can read it?

On the show screen, tap the button with the up and down arrows. The note turns upside down for the person facing you; tap again for Face to face, which shows the note on both halves of the screen, and once more to turn it back.

Can I use it as a cue card or a sign to hold up?

Yes. Show the note in Huge text, or turn the iPhone sideways for one long line, and the screen will not dim or lock while it is shown. Maximum brightness while showing is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. The text does not scroll like a ticker.

How do I keep phrases I use often?

Open Quick Phrases. Four Recommended phrases are included, and Save current note keeps up to 20 of your own under My Phrases, with their colors and text size. Choosing one replaces the note and shows it right away; tap Edit to reorder or delete.

How do I open it quickly from the Lock Screen or Home Screen?

Add the Home Screen widget to see your latest note, or the Lock Screen widget and the Control Center control to jump straight into showing. On an iPhone with an Action button, assign the app's Show in large text control. If the widget looks out of date, tap Reload Widget in Settings.

Is Big Text Note free, and where is my note stored?

It is free with no ads, no account, and no in-app purchases. The note stays on your device, nothing is sent to the developer, and no internet connection is needed. Turn on Open with a blank note in Settings if you want each session to start empty.

Is it a full notes app replacement?

No. Big Text Note keeps one note and is built around showing it in large text, not managing many notes or notebooks.
