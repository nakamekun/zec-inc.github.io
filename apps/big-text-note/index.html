Overview

Big Text Note shows one note in large, easy-to-read text so you can hold it up to the person in front of you. It works as a simple communication board: for talking with someone who is deaf or hard of hearing, for days when your own voice is not available, for noisy places like stations and event halls, or for a quick word at a counter or reception desk.

Type it, show it, and if needed, let the device say it. Read Aloud speaks the note with the voices built into your iPhone or iPad, and voice input turns what the other person says into text on the device. Audio is never saved; only the text you keep is stored.

Show mode makes the note a clear message board with adjustable size and colors, an upside-down flip for the person across the table, and landscape for one long line. Quick Phrases keeps four ready-made lines and up to 20 of your own, one tap away. It works with no ads, no account, and no internet connection, and the app is available in many languages.

Who It Is For

  • People who talk with someone who is deaf or hard of hearing and want a communication board
  • People whose own voice is not available, for example after surgery or when unwell
  • Anyone who needs to get a short message across in a noisy place such as a station or event hall
  • People who want a quick word at a counter or reception desk, or a note to show while traveling

How to Use

  1. Open Big Text Note and type the note you want to show, or use voice input to turn the other person's words into text.
  2. Open Show mode to display the note in large text. Pick Normal, Large, or Huge, then fine-tune the size and choose colors.
  3. Flip the note upside down for the person across the table, or turn your iPhone sideways for one long line.
  4. Use Read Aloud if you want the device to say the note, or tap a Quick Phrase for a ready-made line.
  5. Add your own Quick Phrases (up to 20) and reorder them so the ones you use most are on top.

FAQ

What is Big Text Note for?

It shows one note in large, easy-to-read text so you can hold it up to the person in front of you, as a simple communication board for conversations, noisy places, or quick words at a counter.

Is it a full notes app replacement?

No. Big Text Note is built around one note shown in large text rather than managing many notes or notebooks.

Can it read the note aloud?

Yes. Read Aloud speaks the note with the voices built into your iPhone or iPad. The language is detected from the text, and nothing is sent to a server.

Does it have voice input?

Yes. Voice input turns what the other person says into text on the device. You choose whether to append the result to the note or replace it. Audio is never saved.

Does it require an account?

No. There is no account, no ads, no tracking, and no server sync, and no internet connection is needed. The note stays on your device.

What are Quick Phrases?

Ready-made lines you can show with one tap: four are included, and you can add up to 20 of your own and reorder them.
