Overview

Help Screen SOS is a support app for asking people nearby for help by showing your phone screen when you cannot speak.

The English experience focuses on Silent SOS, personal safety, and travel help. It can show simple high-contrast messages such as “HELP,” “I feel unsafe,” or “I am traveling and need help.” It can also show that you cannot speak, have difficulty speaking, or do not speak the local language.
