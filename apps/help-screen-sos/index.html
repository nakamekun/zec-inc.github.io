Overview

Help Screen SOS is a support app for asking nearby people for help by showing your phone screen when you cannot speak, have difficulty speaking, or do not speak the local language. Choose the situation and show a high-contrast full-screen message.

Messages are available in Japanese, English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Optional alarm sound, vibration, and screen flash begin only after you press the in-app start button. Widgets, Control Center, Shortcuts, and Action Button actions open a safe path into the app rather than starting an alarm immediately.

The app records no audio, takes no photos or videos, sends no location, and does not automatically contact emergency services. It is a communication aid, not a replacement for police, emergency services, station staff, security, or direct help.

Main Features

  • Full-screen Silent SOS and situation-specific messages
  • Manual choice among five display languages
  • Optional explanation that you cannot speak or do not speak the local language
  • Optional English shown together
  • Alarm, vibration, and screen flash controlled from inside the app
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, Shortcuts, and Action Button routes
  • Fully on-device; ready for iPhone Duo

Who It Is For

  • People who cannot safely or easily speak in a difficult situation
  • Travelers who need to show a help message in a local language
  • People preparing a silent SOS message in advance

How to Use

  1. Choose the situation and display language.
  2. Add an optional introductory or English line.
  3. Show the full-screen message to someone nearby.
  4. Only if appropriate, press the in-app button to start alarm, vibration, or flash.

Privacy

Everything runs on the device. The app does not record audio, take photos or videos, send location data, or automatically contact emergency services.

FAQ

Does it contact emergency services?

No. It only shows messages and optional effects you start. Contact emergency services or nearby authorities directly when needed.

Does a widget start the alarm?

No. Widgets and actions open the app; the alarm starts only after you press the in-app button.

Which languages are included?

Japanese, English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
