Overview

Packed is a quick check you do at the door every morning, so nothing gets left behind. Make a group for each routine you repeat — your commute, school bag, or gym — and just check it before you leave. You can check items right from the Home Screen widget, without opening the app.

Every check resets on its own the next day, so there is nothing to clear. Instead of acting like a general task manager, Packed stays close to the packing workflow, and it is handy before a trip, too.

Main Features

  • Check items from the Home Screen widget, and mark a whole group packed in one tap
  • Ready lists to start from: commute, school, gym, overnight trip, business trip, doctor's visit, and emergency bag (the items are common examples you can change freely)
  • Reminders by group, on the days and time you choose (for example weekdays for work, Mon/Wed/Fri for the gym)
  • Reorder items, add counts (×2), and short notes
  • Make as many groups as you like
  • Checks reset automatically every day
  • No account, no ads, no subscription; lists are stored on your device

Who It Is For

  • People who ask themselves "keys? wallet?" every morning before heading out
  • Commuters, students, and gym-goers with a routine bag to pack
  • Families preparing bags for school, trips, or outings
  • Travelers and business travelers who want a ready packing list
  • Users who prefer a simple dedicated checklist over a large productivity app

How to Use

  1. Make a group for a routine you repeat, such as your commute, school bag, or gym, or start from a ready list.
  2. Edit the items, and add counts or short notes if you need them.
  3. Set a reminder for the days and time you leave, and add the widget to your Home Screen.
  4. Before you leave, check items from the widget or the app, or mark the whole group packed in one tap.
  5. The next day, every check resets on its own, ready for the next time.

FAQ

What is Packed for?

It is a quick check you do at the door, for commute, school, gym, or trip essentials, so nothing gets left behind.

Is Packed a general to-do app?

No. Packed is designed around packing and preparation rather than broad project management.

Can families use Packed?

Yes. It is useful for family outings, school bags, children’s items, and repeating routines.

Do I need to clear the checks each day?

No. Every check resets automatically the next day, so your lists are ready to use again.

Does Packed support widgets?

Yes. You can check items right from the Home Screen widget without opening the app, and mark a whole group packed in one tap.

Can I get reminders?

Yes. Set a reminder per group on the days and time you choose, such as weekdays for work or Mon/Wed/Fri for the gym.

Does it need an account, and is my data sent anywhere?

No account is needed, and your lists are stored on your device and are not sent to any server.
