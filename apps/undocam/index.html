Overview

UndoCam is a simple rewind-clip maker for iPhone. Choose a video of 15 seconds or less from your Photos library, and UndoCam plays it backwards — turning everyday moments, funny clips, and quick social posts into instant reversed videos you can save or share.

There is no timeline, no filters, and no complicated editing. UndoCam does one thing: it reverses the whole clip. You pick a video, watch the export progress, preview the result in the app, and then save it to Photos or send it anywhere with the share sheet.

Everything is processed on your device. Videos are never uploaded, and there is no account to create. Exports are rendered as stable 1080p/30fps muted video, prioritizing reliable results over preserving 4K or HDR source quality.

Who It Is For

  • People who want a quick reversed-video effect for social posts, Shorts, or Reels
  • Parents and friends capturing funny everyday moments to replay backwards
  • Anyone who finds full video editors too heavy for a single simple effect
  • Users who prefer on-device processing with no upload and no account

Main Features

  • Pick a short video (15 seconds or less) from your Photos library
  • Reverse the entire clip into a rewind video
  • Export progress display while processing
  • In-app preview of the reversed result
  • Save the reversed video to Photos
  • Share directly through the iOS share sheet
  • All processing happens on your device — no upload, no account

How to Use

  1. Open UndoCam and pick a video from your Photos library.
  2. Choose a clip that is 15 seconds or shorter (longer videos are rejected with a message).
  3. Wait a moment while the app reverses the video on your device.
  4. Preview the rewind clip in the app.
  5. Save it to Photos or share it with the share sheet.

FAQ

Is UndoCam free?

Yes. UndoCam is free with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads. Pricing on the App Store can change, so check the listing for current details.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. All video processing happens on your device. UndoCam does not upload videos, does not require an account, and does not use analytics.

How long can the video be?

UndoCam accepts videos of 15 seconds or less. If you choose a longer video, the app asks you to pick a shorter one.

Does the reversed video keep its sound?

No. Reversed clips are exported without audio. UndoCam focuses on the visual rewind effect only.

What quality is the exported video?

Exports are rendered at up to 1080p/30fps in H.264 for stable, reliable results. Original 4K, HDR, or 60fps quality is not preserved.

Does UndoCam change my original video?

No. The reversed clip is saved as a new video. Your original stays untouched in your Photos library.
