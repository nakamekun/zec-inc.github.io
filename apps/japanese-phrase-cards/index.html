Overview

Show polite, natural Japanese to the person in front of you. Staff point to an answer. You tap it, and the card turns around to show the answer in your language.

Travel apps that translate are great until the waiter can't read your screen, the station is noisy, or you have no signal. Japanese Phrase Cards does something simpler: it shows checked Japanese in large type, and gives staff ready-made answers to point to.
