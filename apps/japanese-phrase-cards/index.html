Overview

Japanese Phrase Cards is built for showing, not typing under pressure. Pick a polite Japanese phrase written and checked by a native speaker, show it in large type, and let restaurant, station, hotel, pharmacy, or shop staff point to a prepared Japanese answer. Tap the answer and the card turns around to show it in your language.

A keypad handles prices, minutes, platforms, and exits. Fixed, checked sentences build allergy cards from Japan's food-labeling allergens and dietary needs, health cards with conditions, medicines, drug allergies, and an emergency contact, and hotel cards with a Japanese address. Personal cards stay on your phone.

The app contains 137 phrases across 10 scenes and works offline. Full-screen cards keep the screen awake, landscape makes text larger, Japanese speech can play normally or slowly, and supported devices can translate spoken replies on device after any needed language download.

Main Features

  • 137 offline phrases in 10 travel scenes
  • Staff-facing prepared answers that flip into the traveler's language
  • Number keypad for prices, time, platforms, and exits
  • Native-written polite Japanese with short context where needed
  • Allergy, health, and hotel personal cards stored on device
  • Large landscape cards, Japanese speech and slow speech
  • Optional on-device spoken-reply translation on supported devices
  • No account or data collection

Who It Is For

  • Travelers in Japan who want checked phrases instead of live text entry
  • People who need to communicate allergies or dietary needs
  • Travelers showing taxi, hotel, pharmacy, station, restaurant, or emergency information

How to Use

  1. Pick a phrase for the situation.
  2. Show the Japanese card and let staff point to an answer or provide a number.
  3. Tap the answer or enter the number to see it in your language.
  4. Create allergy, health, or hotel cards in advance when useful.

Pricing

Allergy, health, and emergency phrases, the first phrases in every scene, and one card of each kind are free. An optional one-time purchase, not a subscription, unlocks every phrase and unlimited cards. Check the App Store for current terms.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

Phrase cards work offline. Personal allergy, health, and hotel cards stay on the phone. The app requires no account and states that it collects no data. Spoken-reply translation is on-device on supported devices, after any language download required by iOS.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes. The 137 fixed phrase cards work offline. Optional spoken-reply translation may require downloading a language first.

Is the full app a subscription?

No. The full unlock is an optional one-time purchase.

Does an allergy card guarantee a food is safe?

No. It helps you ask clearly but cannot guarantee ingredients or preparation. For severe allergies, follow professional medical advice.

Where are my personal cards stored?

On your phone. The app requires no account and states that it collects no data.
