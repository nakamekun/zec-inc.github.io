Overview

Number Forge is a daily number puzzle. Every day brings one new puzzle: combine six numbers with + - x / to forge the target number.

HOW TO PLAY - Pick two tiles and an operation to forge them into a new number. - Reach the target in as few operations as you can. - Par shows the minimum possible. Can you match it?
