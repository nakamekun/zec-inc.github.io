Overview

Number Forge is a daily number puzzle. Every day brings exactly one new puzzle: combine six numbers using addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to forge the target number. Pick two tiles and an operation, forge them into a new number, and work your way to the target in as few operations as you can.

Because there is only one puzzle per day and everyone in the world gets the same one, it becomes a short daily brain habit rather than an endless time sink. Par shows the minimum possible number of operations, so even after you solve it there's a reason to think harder. Once you solve it, see where you stand on the optional Game Center daily leaderboard (scores start at 2,000), and look for other ways to forge the target to raise your title for the day. Streaks, par-match counts, and spoiler-free result sharing keep the habit going.

Not quite there? You can finish whenever you like: within 10 of the target earns 2 stars and within 25 earns 1 star, and stars keep your streak going. Once you have finished, you can see an example solution, and finishing today's puzzle also unlocks practice on earlier dates. A Home Screen widget (small and medium) shows today's target before you solve, or your result after, and an optional once-a-day reminder helps you keep the chain. The app and widget are available in the 13 App Store languages.

  • Stats: solved days, current streak, longest streak, and par matches
  • Spoiler-free share text with puzzle number, operation count, and streak
  • Small and medium Home Screen widgets showing today's target or your solved result

  • No account, no ads, no tracking; the app and widget are available in 13 languages

How to Use

  1. Open the app - today's puzzle appears immediately, no onboarding, no menus.
  2. Pick two number tiles and an operation to forge them into a new number.
  3. Keep combining until you hit the target; use undo or reset if you take a wrong turn. If you cannot get there, you can finish whenever you like and still earn stars.
  4. Try to match par - the minimum possible number of operations.
  5. After solving, check your place on the Daily Forge leaderboard (optional Game Center sign-in), look for other ways to reach the target, and share your result as spoiler-free text.
  6. Come back tomorrow for the next puzzle and keep your streak alive - the widget shows the new target at a glance.

Privacy

No account is required, and there are no ads and no tracking. Your progress - solved days, streaks, and settings - is stored locally on your device (shared with the widget via an App Group, on-device only), with no iCloud sync and no server of our own. The optional daily reminder is a local notification.

FAQ

Is Number Forge free?

Yes. The app is free with no ads and no in-app purchases. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

How does the daily puzzle work?

Each day there is one new puzzle, generated from the date, and every player in the world gets the same one. You combine six numbers with + - x / to reach the target number.

What is par?

Par is the minimum possible number of operations for that day's puzzle. Solving is one thing - matching par is the real challenge, and the app counts your par matches over time.

How does the daily leaderboard work?

After you solve today's puzzle, you can see "Today: #N of M" on the Game Center Daily Forge leaderboard. Scores start at 2,000, minus 200 per move over par and 1 per second (time stops counting at 20 minutes). Game Center sign-in is optional, and everything works without it.

What if I cannot reach the target?

You can finish whenever you like. Within 10 of the target earns 2 stars and within 25 earns 1 star, and stars keep your streak going. Afterwards you can see an example solution.

Can I play earlier puzzles?

Yes. After you finish today's puzzle, you can practice earlier dates on your device. Practice never affects rankings, streaks, titles, or stats.

Does it work offline?

Puzzles are generated on your device, so you can play offline. Only the optional Game Center leaderboard needs a connection.

Is there a widget?

Yes, in small and medium sizes. Before you solve, it shows today's target with a quick way into the game; after you solve, it shows your result. It updates at midnight for the next puzzle.

What data does the app collect?

There is no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your streaks and results stay on your device. Only if you sign in to Game Center, your daily score is sent to Game Center for the leaderboard.

Will it send me notifications?

Only if you turn on the optional once-a-day reminder. The app works fully without notifications.
