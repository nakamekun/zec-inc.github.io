Why This App

Foot Size Check is for parents who want to measure a child’s feet at home with paper and an iPhone, then keep a record as they grow. Trace each foot on Letter or A4 paper, photograph the sheet, and the app places the length and width points automatically; you can adjust them if needed.

A ruler or printed measuring guide can help with a single measurement. Foot Size Check adds separate left and right records, saved foot outlines, growth graphs and reminders, so you can compare with earlier measurements without making your own chart.

Measuring and recording are free, with no account or banner ads. Photo analysis happens on your device; syncing records to your own iCloud is optional. Shoe sizes are estimates, so check the fit by trying shoes on before buying.

Main Features

  • Foot measurement on Letter or A4 paper: trace the foot, photograph the sheet, and get length and width automatically (adjust by hand if you like)
  • Foot outline saved as a true-size shape, with a foot outline card to send to family right after saving
  • Suggested shoe size (foot length plus 0.5-1.0 cm), EU, US, and UK conversion, and your own notation (JP, EU, US, or UK in Settings)
  • Shoe size chart for children, men, and women
  • Growth history over time, left and right feet tracked separately, and a growth card to share as one image
  • Insole measuring to see the current toe room, and how-to-fit-shoes checks based on podiatry society guidance
  • Find shoes by age and sex or by use, My size book, seasonal shoe reminders, and age-based measurement reminders (Japan; links include advertising)
  • Optional iCloud sync to your own iCloud; no account, no banner ads
  • Foot Size Check Pro (optional one-time purchase): same-age reference band on the growth chart, shoe replacement estimate, more than one child and shoe note, full "All" growth span, show-in-store large text, and more

How to Use

  1. Choose Letter or A4 paper, place a sheet on a flat floor, and stand the foot on it.
  2. Trace around the foot with a pen, then photograph the sheet.
  3. The app reads the outline and places the heel, toe, and width points for you. Fine-tune them by hand if needed.
  4. Read the measured foot length, width, and suggested shoe size, and save the record to build a growth history (left and right feet separately).
  5. Share the foot outline card or growth card with family, or turn on measurement reminders for the next time.

Who It Suits

Best for

  • Parents checking foot length before choosing a child’s next pair of shoes.
  • Families keeping separate left and right measurements and comparing growth over time.
  • People who want measurement reminders and no app account.

Not for

  • For foot girth or instep measurements, use a tape measure or an in-store measuring service.
  • For medical or specialist sports footwear measurements, consult a qualified clinician or specialist fitter.
  • To decide by trying shoes on immediately, use an in-store fitting service.
  • If you do not want to trace on paper, use a shop’s measuring equipment instead.

FAQ

How do I measure my child’s foot size at home without a ruler or foot gauge?

You need Letter or A4 paper, a pen and your iPhone. Trace the foot, lift it off the paper and photograph the sheet from above. Foot Size Check reads the outline and estimates length and width; check the points and adjust them if needed.

How can I measure foot length with paper and my iPhone camera?

Choose the paper size in Foot Size Check, place the paper on a flat floor and trace the foot. Photograph the whole sheet so the app can detect its corners. Save each foot separately to keep a history.

What shoe size should my toddler wear for a 14 cm foot length?

Foot Size Check uses foot length plus 0.5–1.0 cm as a label-size guide and provides EU, US and UK conversions. There is no single size that fits every brand or foot shape. Use the guide as a starting point and try the shoes on.

How often should I measure my kid’s feet, and how fast do children’s feet grow?

Growth varies, so compare your child’s saved measurements rather than assuming a fixed growth rate. Turn on measurement reminders; with a birth date entered, the app uses age-based intervals. Without one, you can choose every 1, 2, 3 or 6 months.

How can I check how much toe room my child’s shoes have?

Trace the removable insole on paper and photograph it using the insole measurement feature. The app compares insole length with the saved foot length to show estimated toe room. Check the actual fit as well; the result is a guide.

How do I track my child’s foot growth on iPhone?

Save measurements for the left and right foot separately. The growth graph and saved outlines let you compare records over time, and a growth card can be shared with family.

Is Foot Size Check free, or does it need a subscription?

Measurement, records, growth graphs, outlines and reminders are free. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription, for extras such as additional children and same-age reference bands. Current terms appear in the App Store.

Does a foot-measuring app upload my photos or need an account?

Foot Size Check needs no app account and analyses measurement photos on your device. Optional iCloud sync saves records and outlines to your own private iCloud storage; annotated measurement photos remain on the device.
