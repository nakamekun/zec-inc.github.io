Plant Tap: Watering Reminder

See which houseplants need water today, log watering in one tap, and keep dated growth photos with optional reminders and widgets.

Overview

Add each plant with a name, optional photo, watering cycle, and last-watered date. Plant Tap calculates the next date and keeps a simple local history, while dated photos build a growth album.

Optional reminders and Home or Lock Screen widgets show what is due. You can mark a plant watered from a widget, Siri, or Shortcuts without opening the app. Records and photos stay on your device with no account, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Main Features

  • Per-plant watering cycle and calculated next date
  • One-tap local watering history
  • Dated growth-photo album
  • Optional reminders and Home and Lock Screen widgets
  • Log from a widget, Siri, or Shortcuts
  • No account, ads, analytics, or tracking

Who It Is For

  • Houseplant owners who want a simple watering schedule
  • People who want dated growth photos without an account
  • Widget and Siri users who want one-tap logging

How to Use

  1. Add a plant with its watering cycle and last-watered date.
  2. Check what is due today and tap when you water it.
  3. Optionally add dated growth photos and reminders or widgets.

Pricing

Up to 3 plants and viewing their current photos are free. Plant Tap Pro is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription for unlimited plants and continued additions to each growth album; eligible new subscribers may receive a one-week trial. Saved plants, photos, and records remain viewable if Pro ends. Check the App Store for current terms.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

Plant records and photos stay on the device. The app requires no account and has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

FAQ

What is free?

You can track up to 3 plants and view their current photos. Pro adds unlimited plants and continued growth-album additions.

What happens if Pro ends?

Saved plants, photos, and records stay on the device and remain viewable.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, with no account, ads, analytics, or tracking.
