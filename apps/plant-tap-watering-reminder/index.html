Overview

Know which houseplants need water today and record it with one tap.

Add each plant with a name, optional photo, watering cycle, and last-watered date. Plant Tap calculates the next date and keeps a simple local history. Add dated photos to each plant's growth album to revisit changes over time. Optional reminders and Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets help you check what is due. You can also mark a plant as watered from the widget, Siri, or Shortcuts without opening the app.
