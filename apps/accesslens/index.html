Access Lens: Magnifier

A private camera magnifier for small print, with zoom, light, freeze, and high-contrast reading modes. Color-view comparison remains available.

Overview

Access Lens opens straight into a camera magnifier for small print such as medicine leaflets, receipts, ingredient labels, menus, and signs. Magnify with 2×, 4×, or 8× buttons where supported, a slider, or pinch, and turn on the light in dim places.

Freeze the view to read at your own pace, then pinch and drag the frozen image. Invert, black-on-yellow, high-contrast, and grayscale modes can make text easier to separate from its background. The magnifier never saves an image, and a frozen view is discarded when you close it or leave the app.

The earlier Compare Views tool remains one tap away. It compares a camera view or photo side by side with color-vision, grayscale, blur, narrow-field, or low-contrast approximations, and lets you save or share the comparison when you choose. It is an awareness tool, not a medical device. The app is available in 25 languages.

Main Features

  • Camera magnifier opens immediately, with 2×, 4× and 8× buttons where supported, a slider, and pinch zoom
  • Light for dim places
  • Freeze the view, then pinch and drag the frozen image
  • Reading modes: invert, black on yellow, high contrast, and grayscale
  • The magnifier saves nothing; frozen views are discarded when you close or leave the app
  • Compare Views: side-by-side color-vision, grayscale, blur, narrow-field, and low-contrast approximations for camera views or photos

  • No account, ads, or network; available in 25 languages on iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape

Who It Is For

  • People who want to enlarge small print on labels, receipts, menus, or signs
  • People who find high-contrast or inverted text easier to read
  • Anyone who wants a magnifier that does not save camera images
  • Designers, teachers, and accessibility advocates using the optional Compare Views tool

How to Use

  1. Open Access Lens; the camera magnifier starts immediately.
  2. Choose 2×, 4×, or 8× where supported, use the slider, or pinch to set the magnification.
  3. Turn on the light if needed, or choose invert, black on yellow, high contrast, or gray.
  4. Tap Freeze to stop the view, then pinch and drag while you read. Closing or leaving discards it.
  5. For accessibility comparison, open Compare Views at the top and choose a camera view or photo.

Privacy

The magnifier saves no images to Photos, and a frozen view is discarded when you close it or leave the app. Camera frames and photos used in Compare Views are processed on your device; only a comparison you explicitly save or share leaves the screen. Access Lens has no account, ads, analytics, or network connection.

FAQ

Is Access Lens free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no account. App Store pricing can change, so check the listing for current details.

Are my camera images or photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Camera frames and selected photos are processed on your device. Access Lens has no server, no analytics, and no account, and saving and sharing happen only when you choose them.

Do the filters accurately reproduce vision conditions?

No. The results are approximations for awareness and accessibility discussions. They do not exactly reproduce any individual experience, and Access Lens is not a medical tool.

What filters are included?

Color approximations for Type 1 (P), Type 2 (D), Type 3 (T), and grayscale, plus visual filters for blur and cloudy view, narrow field and center spot, and low contrast. Strength is adjustable where supported.

Can I use it without a camera?

Yes. You can choose a photo or start with one of the five built-in samples instead of using the live camera.

Can I save or share the result?

Yes. Saving and sharing happen only when you choose them.
