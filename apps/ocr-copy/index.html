Image to Text: OCR Copy

Copy text from photos, screenshots, documents, and PDFs. Read many images, preserve tables, search a private on-device library, and export searchable PDFs.

Why This App

OCR Copy is for people who want all the text in photos or screenshots copied at once, without selecting it by hand. It reads several images in order and lets you combine the results or keep each page separate.

For a short passage in one photo, iPhone Live Text can be enough. OCR Copy brings together batch reading, table export to CSV, optional joining of broken lines, and a searchable library when you want to reuse more than a snippet.

Images and text are processed and stored on your device, with no account, ads or tracking. Five reads a day are free; Pro removes the reading limit.

Main Features

  • Read multiple photos or screenshots as one combined result or one result per image; Pro selects up to 100, sharing up to 20, and free use up to the remaining daily reads
  • Document camera with automatic capture and skew correction, saved as a searchable PDF
  • Read text PDFs directly and scanned PDFs page by page, up to 50 pages at a time
  • Preserve table rows and columns for CSV, and optionally join broken lines into paragraphs
  • Highlight uncertain lines and, on supported iPhones, review corrections suggested by on-device AI
  • Scan Live and direct reading from the share sheet and Shortcuts
  • On-device Library with titles, groups, normalized full-text search, and optional iPhone Search
  • Export the whole Library as text, searchable PDF, or ZIP; lock it with Face ID
  • Images and text are processed and stored on device, with no account, ads, or tracking
  • Free: 5 reads a day and 3 groups. OCR Copy Pro removes both limits; earlier users who had unlimited reading keep it
  • App available in 25 languages

How to Use

  1. Open OCR Copy and choose photos or screenshots (up to 20 at a time), live scan, or camera capture. You can also tap Share in Photos or right after a screenshot and choose OCR Copy.
  2. Let the app read the text on your device. The text is copied to your clipboard as soon as it is read.
  3. Paste it into notes, messages, or search with one tap.
  4. If needed, tap a line to fix or delete it before copying.
  5. Save the text to review and copy again later from the Home screen, or export a table as CSV.
  6. If text isn't reading well, change the OCR language or accuracy in Settings.

Who It Suits

Best for

  • People copying whole screenshots or long passages without dragging selection handles.
  • People combining text from several photos or exporting a table as CSV.
  • People who want to search saved text without uploading images to a server.

Not for

  • If you only need one line from a photo, use iPhone Live Text.
  • If translation or reading aloud is the goal, use Google Lens for translation or Copy Text On Screen for read-aloud tools.
  • If signatures and automatic cloud export matter most, consider QuickScan or Adobe Scan.
  • For vertical text or complex layouts that OCR Copy may misread, use a specialist OCR tool and check the result.

Pricing

OCR Copy is free to download with 5 reads a day and up to 3 Library groups. Text PDFs, copying, the Library, and exports do not count as reads. OCR Copy Pro is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription that removes the read limit in the app, share sheet, and Shortcuts, allows up to 100 selected images, and removes the group limit; eligible yearly subscribers may receive a one-week trial. People who used OCR Copy before version 1.5 keep unlimited reading. See the App Store for current terms.

Pricing and availability can vary by country and may change on the App Store.

Privacy

OCR runs entirely on your iPhone with Apple Vision, works offline, and photos and text are never sent to a server. From version 1.8, images you are working on (including straightened document images) are kept temporarily on your device and deleted when they are no longer needed: when a read fails or is redone, when you close the result, or when you delete it from history; anything left over is removed the next time the app starts. Suggested fixes for misread text are made by Apple's on-device AI on supported iPhones, and neither photos nor text leave your device. Saved history keeps recognized text, line corrections, confidence values, and thumbnails locally in the app. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking. Sharing text is always user-initiated through the iOS share sheet. Subscription purchases are handled by the App Store and your Apple Account.

FAQ

How do I copy all the text from a screenshot on iPhone without selecting it?

Share the screenshot to OCR Copy to recognise and copy its text. You can also open the image in the app. Review the result before using it.

How can I extract text from multiple images at once on iPhone?

Select several images and choose to combine them or keep each page separate. The share sheet and Shortcuts accept up to 20 images per run; free reading is subject to the daily limit.

How do I turn a photo of a table into a CSV or spreadsheet on iPhone?

When a table is recognised, use Export Table as CSV. Rows and columns are preserved for opening in Numbers or Excel. This feature requires iOS 26 or later.

Is there an OCR app for iPhone that works offline without uploading my photos?

OCR Copy recognises text on your iPhone and stores images and text locally. It has no account, ads or tracking. Suggested corrections also run on-device on supported iPhones.

How do I remove unwanted line breaks when copying text from a book page?

Turn on the option to join broken lines in OCR Copy; it is off by default. It joins wrapped lines into a paragraph. Check the result, especially with complex layouts or vertical text.

How do I make a scanned PDF searchable on iPhone?

Import the PDF in OCR Copy. Existing text is extracted directly and scanned pages are read with OCR, up to 50 pages per import. Export a searchable PDF from the result.

Is OCR Copy free, and how much is unlimited OCR?

Five reads a day are free and reset at midnight, with up to three Library groups. Text PDFs, copying, the Library and exports do not count. Pro removes the read and group limits and allows up to 100 selected images; eligible annual subscribers may receive a trial. Current terms appear in the App Store.
