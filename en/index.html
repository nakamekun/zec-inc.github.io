HOW WE MAKE

“Wouldn’t it be nice if…”, built small and fast.

Not one big app that does everything, but many small tools that each do one thing right away.

Runs on your device

Your records stay on your iPhone by default. Syncing between devices is left to Apple’s own services such as iCloud.

No guilt, no rush

Missed a day? The app won’t scold you. Notifications come only when they’re needed, in a gentle tone.

Improved after launch

We read reviews and watch how the apps are used, and ship updates almost every week. Small apps are quick to fix.
